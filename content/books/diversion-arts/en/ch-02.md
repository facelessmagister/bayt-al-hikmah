The Qur'an - the aesthetic miracle:
   Definitely, the Glorious Qur'an is the greatest sign which
Islam brought forth. It is the greatest miracle conveyed by the
Prophet. It is both an aesthetic and intellectual miracle.
Besides the fact that the Arabs were famous for their mastery
of rhetoric. They stood helpless before the unrivalled rhetoric,
versification, style, rhyme and rhythm of Qur'an, to such an
extent that they suspected it was written by power of magic.
   The rhetorical or aesthetic miracle of Qur'an is a topic that
has stimulated the interest of the professors of rhetoric and
Arabic through out the years, starting with Adel Kahr,
Arrafeiae, Sayd Kotob, and ending with the writings of Bent
Ash-Shatea and other contemporary scholars.
   The charismatic power of the Qur'an comes into play when
the beauty of rhetoric and rhyme intermingles with the beauty
of
 distinct recitation. Hence Allahis, the Most High, words:
   "And recite the Qur'an with distinct recitation".
