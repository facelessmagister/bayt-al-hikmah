# Critiquing the Current Reality

It is fitting for the caller (*al-dāʿiyah*, الداعية), in his first meetings with the new elements he intends to call to Islam, to raise the subject of critiquing the prevailing conditions under which the Ummah is living, and to clarify—so far as he can—his critique with objectivity, figures, and logic. The more he is able to touch upon the ills and diseases, to expose their causes and motives, and to explain their effects and consequences, the deeper will be his impact and the greater his influence upon those present.

I believe the caller should address this subject through the following points:

## First: The Corruption of Creed

The first catastrophe to befall the Ummah was the corruption of her *ʿaqīdah* (عقيدة) and her affliction with grievous materialist taints that planted doubt, materialist concepts, and irreligious opinions, thereby exposing the spirit of religiosity to violent and terrifying shocks. These shocks later paved the way for the emergence of atheistic trends (*ittijāhāt ilḥādīyyah*, اتجاهات إلحادية) and their dominance over the lands of the Muslims and their resources, and then for their war against Islam and its callers—at times by spreading rumors and fabrications, and at other times by inventing pretexts and justifications to uproot the callers and be rid of them.

## Second: Moral Deviation

One result of the corruption of *ʿaqīdah* that afflicted the Ummah was the collapse of her morals and the spread of immorality and vice among her.

Historical evidence confirms, beyond doubt, that among the causes of the fall and disappearance of nations is their immersion in the mire of vices and lusts, and their turning to pleasures and gratifications. Allah, the Exalted, says:

> "Indeed, those who like that immorality should be spread among those who believe will have a painful punishment in this world and the Hereafter." [Qur'an, An-Nur 24:19]

## Third: Failed Attempts at Liberation

This Ummah has witnessed, over the past quarter-century, failed attempts at liberation.

On the political level, the nationalist trends failed to establish any form of unity or union, even between two Arab countries.

On the economic level, the imported theories—capitalism and socialism—failed to produce a society of sufficiency and justice. Indeed, these theories dealt a deathblow to the national economy, leading to the destruction of commercial and financial life in more than one country.

As for the military level, the successive defeats suffered by the Ummah confirm, beyond doubt, the inability of the existing regimes to prepare the Ummah psychologically and materially to confront the colonialist challenges and to wage the battles of liberation.

## Fourth: The State of Loss Afflicting the Ummah

Thus the Ummah has come to be in a state of loss—utter loss. The conditions she lives in are extremely abnormal and deviant. Catastrophes and disasters descend upon her from every side.

The Ummah would not have reached what she has reached in vain. It is the harvest of her turning away from Allah, her rebellion against His sovereignty, her denial of His *manhaj* (منهج) in life, and consequently her return to the first *jāhiliyyah* (جاهلية) all over again.

## If the Cause Is Known, Wonder Ceases

The truth is that this relapse (*riddah*) that has afflicted the Muslims was the natural result of the conspiracies in which the tools of destruction collaborated against this Ummah from every quarter: from Shuʿūbiyyah (شعوبية) movements to materialist trends, from Zionist schemes to Masonic lodges, from foreign intelligence pens, and from the material resources and media apparatuses possessed by these forces and trends.

The Ummah has suffered grievously because of this and endured the utmost hardship, and she has seen the proof of it in every aspect of her life. Her social life has become putrid from the abundance of immoralities and abominations that have spread through it. Her economic life has become barren because of the failure of the man-made ruling systems and their inability to achieve justice and prosperity.

She has likewise seen the proof of it in her weakness, the disunity of her word, her division, and then her defeat in successive battles against a deformed state and a gang of deviants from every horizon.

Finally, after the materialist trends and man-made doctrines have gone bankrupt in solving the Ummah's problems and treating the impulses and deviations sweeping over her, Islam remains the only *manhaj* qualified to confront the challenges of materialist civilization, and the only orientation qualified to lead this Ummah—indeed, to nominate her for the leadership of humanity.

For the tyrannical materialism that has trampled under its path every ideal and value, and that has planted anxiety, disturbance, deviation, and abnormality throughout the inhabited world, cannot be confronted, repelled, and uprooted by anything except the logic of the *fitrah* (فطرة), for it is stronger than materialism and more capable of making man happy and providing him with tranquility.

The Ummah—after she has exhausted her precious energies in the whirlpool of her failed experiments, and after she has reached the misery and hardship she has reached because of her turning away from the voice of the *fitrah*—is called anew to express her original identity, called to appear before the world with the message of rescue and salvation, called to realize her Islamic self in the battle of self-realization.

And here it is fitting for the caller to critique the two global doctrines.
