# Introduction

Those working in the Islamic field today are in the utmost need of a *manhaj* (method) of *tablīgh* (conveyance) to which they adhere, and of intellectual and activist starting-points from which they proceed in the sphere of calling people to Islam. They need all of this to be of such precision and clarity that they can clearly see the path, its characteristics, and its conditions, so that they no longer wander blindly, or do harm to Islam while thinking that they are doing good.

Therefore, they need a *manhaj* that defines for them the path of *daʿwah* to Allah, how to address people and convince them, and the topics that are best raised before them and explained in accessible terms.

In my view, this *manhaj* consists of four sections:

**The First Section:**

It revolves around the obligation of *daʿwah* to Allah: that faith in Islam entails working for it, calling to it, and *jihād* in its cause. This in turn entails the establishment of a collective activist work; individual effort will not produce an Islamic reality or realize an Islamic society.

**The Second Section:**

It is a presentation of the style that should be adopted in calling people to Islam. The callers must address people according to their capacity to respond, and according to their level of understanding. Their guiding policy in that must be knowledge, wisdom, and sound conduct.

**The Third Section:**

It is a rapid overview of the major outlines upon which the Islamic *manhaj* rests, whether in the sphere of *ʿaqīdah* (creed) or in the sphere of *sharīʿah* (Divine Law). Taken together, these constitute the topics that are best raised for discussion before those whom one intends to call.

**The Fourth and Final Section:**

As for the fourth and final section, it is an affirmation of the necessity of establishing collective activist work for Islam. This collective activist work is the path the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, followed in building the first Islamic community. Every individual effort for Islam is bound to be lost unless it is tied to an activist organization. All of this obliges the believers in Islam to establish an activist organization, or to join an existing one.

As I offer this treatise to my brothers, the callers in every place, I ask Allah, the Exalted and Majestic, to record the reward, erase the mistakes, and realize the hope.

> And indeed, Allah is the Guide of those who have believed to a straight path. [Qur'an, Al-Hajj 22:54]
