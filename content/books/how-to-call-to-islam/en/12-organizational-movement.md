# The Necessity of Organizational Movement

## Fourth Investigation: The Necessity of Organizational Movement and the Obligation of Working Within It

In this section the caller should address the subject of working to resume Islamic life and establish Islamic rule, affirming that it is a sharʿī obligation. He should make clear that working to resume Islamic life and establish Islamic rule necessitates the existence of an activist organization, or joining an Islamic activist organization.

The caller may approach this subject through the following points:

### The Messenger’s Work Was Activist, Not Individual

One of the things that confirms the necessity of establishing an activist organization for Islam, as the path to resuming Islamic life and establishing Islamic rule, is the method adopted by the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) in his preparation for establishing the Islamic state.

For the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) did not adopt the method of individual work. Rather, from the very first day he was eager to establish an activist organization, choosing its elements with discernment and selecting its members carefully, so that it would be the driving force of the Islamic movement that, in a short time, filled the eye, hearing, and sight of the world. (See the Prophetic biography in the Meccan period.)

### Collective Work Is a Sharʿī Obligation

It should also be said that collective work for Islam is a sharʿī obligation. This is because working to resume Islamic life is a sharʿī obligation, and this obligation is realized only through the establishment of a collective organization. Thus collective effort in working for Islam likewise becomes a sharʿī obligation.

Many texts in the Qur’an and the Sunnah call to cooperation, unity, and joining the ranks.

In the Noble Qur’an, Allah the Exalted says:

> And let there be [arising] from you a nation inviting to [all that is] good, enjoining what is right and forbidding what is wrong, and those will be the successful. [Qur’an, Āl ʿImrān 3:104]

And He says:

> You are the best nation produced [as an example] for mankind. You enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong and believe in Allah. [Qur’an, Āl ʿImrān 3:110]

And He says:

> And it is not for the believers to go forth [to battle] all at once. For there should separate from every division of them a group [remaining] to obtain understanding in the religion and warn their people when they return to them that they might be cautious. [Qur’an, At-Tawbah 9:122]

And He says:

> [Allah] said, “We will strengthen your arm through your brother and grant you both supremacy so they will not reach you. [It will be] through Our signs; you and those who follow you will be the predominant.” [Qur’an, Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:35]

And He says:

> And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression. [Qur’an, Al-Māʾidah 5:2]

In the noble hadith, the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) said:

> “Hold fast to the *jamāʿah* (جماعة), for the *jamāʿah* is mercy and division is punishment.”

And he said:

> “Whoever separates is not of us. The hand of Allah is with the *jamāʿah*, and the wolf eats only the sheep that strays from the flock.” [Sahih Muslim, Book of Government, Hadith 1846]

And he said:

> “Keep to the *jamāʿah* of the Muslims and their *imām*." It was narrated by al-Ṭabarānī in *al-Muʿjam al-Kabīr*.

Ḥudhayfah said: “What if they have no *jamāʿah* and no *imām*?” He said: “Then keep away from all those factions, even if you must bite on the root of a tree until death overtakes you while you are in that state.”

And he said:

> “Whoever dies while separate from the *jamāʿah* dies a death of *jāhiliyyah* (جاهلية).” [Sahih Muslim, Book of Government, Hadith 1848]

### Organization, Pledge of Allegiance, and Leadership

Another point that may be considered evidence for the necessity of the activist organization for Islam is that every action, in the judgment of Islam, requires organization; and every organization, in turn, must have at its head a leader who is obeyed in what is right. Hence numerous sharʿī texts require appointing an *amīr* (أمير) or leader under whose judgment everyone submits, in order to preserve unity of word, order of ranks, and discipline. Calling for the appointment of leaders clearly implies the existence of an organization and a group, for there is no justification for having a leader without a group or a following.

This is what the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) indicated when he said:

> “When three of you set out, appoint one of you as *amīr*." [Sunan Abī Dāwūd, Book of Travel, Hadith 2608]

And he said:

> “Whoever throws off the hand of obedience will meet Allah on the Day of Resurrection without any proof for himself, and whoever dies while having a pledge of allegiance around his neck dies a death of *jāhiliyyah*." [Sahih Muslim, Book of Government, Hadith 1847]

### The Immense Goals of Islam Require an Activist Organization

The caller should also make clear to the one being called that the path to resuming Islamic life and establishing the Islamic state is not realized merely by preaching and guidance, or by moving the tongue in speech and the pen in writing. Rather, this requires preparing all the scientific, material, technical, and human prerequisites and requirements demanded by this immense goal.

Islam is a call of demolition and construction: demolition of the entire *jāhili* society and the erection of the Islamic society in its place. This process of demolition requires burdens that individuals are incapable of bearing. Indeed, nothing can rise to it, despite strenuous effort, except an activist organization equal to the anticipated struggle.

Furthermore, the path of Islamic work is strewn with thorns and surrounded by dangers. The obstacles that block the way are many, the challenges facing Islam and its people are grave, and the problems left behind by materialist civilizations and philosophies demand colossal efforts on every front.

This makes the existence of an activist organization imperative, just as it makes it incumbent upon everyone who believes in Islam as a *manhaj* (منهج) to be part of the nucleus of this organization, its vanguard, and among those who call to it and work to support it, develop it, and expand it.
