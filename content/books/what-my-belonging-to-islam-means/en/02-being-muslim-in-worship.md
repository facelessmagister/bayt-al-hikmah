# Chapter 2: Being a Muslim in My Worship

Worship (*‘ibādah*) in Islam is the fountainhead of submission and the summit of feeling the greatness of the One worshipped. It is the ascending path of connection between the creature and the Creator. It also has profound effects in one’s dealings with Allah’s creation. In this regard the pillars of Islam — prayer, fasting, zakah, and Hajj — and all works by which a person seeks the face of Allah and follows His law, are equal. The logic of Islam insists that life in its entirety be worship and obedience. This is the meaning of His, the Exalted, saying: *“And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me. I want from them no provision, nor do I want them to feed Me. Indeed, it is Allah who is the Provider, the Firm Possessor of Strength.”* [Qur’an, Adh-Dhariyat 51:56–58] And His saying: *“Say, indeed my prayer, my sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds.”* [Qur’an, Al-An‘am 6:162]

So that I may be a Muslim in my worship, this obliges me to the following:

## 1. That My Worship Be Alive, Connected to the Worshipped

This is the level of excellence (*iḥsān*) in worship. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was asked about *iḥsān* and said: *“That you worship Allah as though you see Him; and if you do not see Him, then indeed He sees you.”* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 2, Hadith 47; Sahih Muslim, Book 1, Hadith 1]

## 2. That It Be a Worship of Humility

I should feel in it the warmth of closeness and the sweetness of devotion. ‘Ā’ishah, may Allah be pleased with her, said: *“The Messenger of Allah ﷺ would be joking with us and we with him. Then when prayer came, it was as if he did not know us and we did not know him.”* [Sahih Muslim, Book 4, Hadith 862]

To this the Messenger ﷺ pointed with his saying: *“How many a fasting person has nothing from his fast but hunger and thirst.”* [Sunan Ibn Majah, Book 7, Hadith 1687; authenticated by al-Albani] And his saying: *“How many a praying person has nothing from his prayer but fatigue and exertion.”* [Sunan Ibn Majah, Book 5, Hadith 1437; authenticated by al-Albani]

## 3. That I Be Present of Heart in My Worship

Removed from the occupations of the world around me and its anxieties. To this the Messenger ﷺ pointed with his saying: *“Allah does not look at a prayer in which a person does not bring along his heart with his body.”* [Sunan an-Nasa’i, Book 10, Hadith 1269] And it was said: *“Prayer is of the Hereafter; so when you enter it, you have left the world.”*

It is reported from al-Ḥasan [al-Baṣrī] that he said: *“Every prayer in which the heart is not present is quicker to punishment.”*

## 4. That in Worship I Be Insatiable in Hope and Unsated in Thirst

I draw near to Allah through the supererogatory acts (*nawāfil*) in response to the words of Allah, the Exalted, in the sacred hadith (*ḥadīth qudsī*): *“Whosoever shows enmity to a friend of Mine, I declare war upon him. My servant does not draw near to Me with anything more beloved to Me than what I have made obligatory upon him. And My servant continues to draw near to Me with supererogatory acts until I love him. When I love him, I am his hearing with which he hears, his sight with which he sees, his hand with which he strikes, and his foot with which he walks. If he asks of Me, I will surely give him; and if he seeks refuge in Me, I will surely protect him. I do not hesitate regarding anything I am to do as I hesitate to take the soul of a believer who hates death, for I dislike displeasing him.”* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 81, Hadith 6502]

## 5. That I Be Eager for the Night Vigil (*Qiyām al-Layl*)

And that I train my soul upon it until it grows accustomed to it. For the night vigil is among the strongest generators of faith. And Allah, the Exalted, spoke truly when He said: *“Indeed, the hours of the night are more effective for concurrence [of heart and tongue] and more suitable for words.”* [Qur’an, Al-Muzzammil 73:6] And He described His believing servants by His saying: *“They used to sleep but little of the night, and in the hours before dawn they would ask forgiveness.”* [Qur’an, Adh-Dhariyat 51:17–18] And *“Their sides part [i.e., they arise] from [their] beds; they supplicate their Lord in fear and aspiration, and from what We have provided them, they spend.”* [Qur’an, As-Sajdah 32:16]

## 6. That I Have Sessions and Contemplations with the Noble Qur’an

Especially at dawn, for He, the Exalted, said: *“Indeed, the Qur’an of dawn is ever witnessed.”* [Qur’an, Al-Isrā’ 17:78] I recite it with reflection, thought, humility, and sorrow, for the Messenger ﷺ said: *“Indeed, this Qur’an was sent down with sorrow; so when you recite it, show sorrow.”* [Reported by Abū Ya‘lā and Abū Nu‘aym in *al-Ḥilyah*] And also because I must remember the saying of Allah, the Exalted: *“If We had sent down this Qur’an upon a mountain, you would have seen it humble itself, splitting from fear of Allah.”* [Qur’an, Al-Ḥashr 59:21] And the saying of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ: *“No one truly believes in the Qur’an who considers lawful what it has declared unlawful.”* [Sunan al-Bayhaqī, Shu‘ab al-Īmān] And his saying: *“The best act of worship of my Ummah is the recitation of the Qur’an.”* [Reported by al-Ḥākim]

And in the hadith of ‘Abdullah ibn Mas‘ūd, may Allah be pleased with him, from the Prophet ﷺ who said: *“Indeed, this Qur’an is the banquet of Allah; so accept His banquet as much as you are able. This Qur’an is a rope whose one end is in the hand of Allah and the other end is in your hands; so hold fast to it, for you will not go astray nor be destroyed. And it will not wear out from much recitation. Recite it, for Allah will reward you for every letter with ten good deeds. I do not say that Alif Lām Mīm is one letter, but rather alif is a letter, lām is a letter, and mīm is a letter.”* [Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Book 9, Hadith 2910; authenticated by al-Albani]

And in his counsel to Ibn ‘Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both: *“Hold fast to the recitation of the Qur’an, for it is a light for you on earth and a treasure for you in heaven.”* [Reported by al-Ḥākim]

The following are some of the supererogatory acts that are recommended to persevere in and to do much of: the night vigil, the *Ḍuḥā* prayer, the *Tarāwīḥ* prayer, fasting — especially the day of ‘Arafah for non-pilgrims, the day of ‘Āshūrā’, six days of Shawwāl, the white days [the 13th, 14th, and 15th] of every month, and *i‘tikāf* (spiritual retreat).

## 7. That Supplication (*Du‘ā’*) Be My Ascent to Allah in Every Matter of Mine

For supplication is the essence of worship. And I should be eager for the reported supplications. Allah spoke truly when He said: *“Call upon Me; I will respond to you.”* [Qur’an, Ghāfir 40:60]

Among these reported supplications are:

- **At sleep:** *“In Your name, my Lord, I lie down, and in Your name I rise. If You take my soul, forgive it; and if You release it, protect it as You protect Your righteous servants.”* [Sahih Muslim, Book 48, Hadith 2713]
- **Upon waking:** *“All praise is for Allah who gave us life after He caused us to die, and to Him is the return.”* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 80, Hadith 6324]
- **When putting on and taking off a garment:** *“O Allah, I ask You for the good of it and the good for which it was made, and I seek refuge in You from the evil of it and the evil for which it was made.”* [Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Book 5, Hadith 3455]
- **When leaving and entering the home:** *“In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no power nor strength except with Allah.”* [Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Book 6, Hadith 3518; Sunan Abu Dawud, Book 39, Hadith 5095]
- **When going to the mosque:** *“O Allah, place light in my heart, light in my sight, light in my hearing, light on my right, light on my left, light above me, light below me, light in front of me, light behind me, and grant me light.”* [Sahih Muslim, Book 4, Hadith 763]
- **Upon entering the mosque:** *“O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.”* And when leaving, let him say: *“O Allah, I ask You of Your bounty.”* [Sahih Muslim, Book 4, Hadith 713]
- **At food:** *“O Allah, bless what You have provided us and save us from the punishment of the Fire.”* [Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Book 5, Hadith 3442] And: *“In the name of Allah.”* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 10, Hadith 5376]
- **At the end of eating:** *“All praise is for Allah who fed us and gave us drink and made us Muslims.”* [Sunan Abu Dawud, Book 34, Hadith 3850]
- **When entering the lavatory:** *“O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the male and female devils.”* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 4, Hadith 142]
- **When leaving the lavatory:** *“All praise is for Allah who made its delight reach me and kept away from me its harm.”* [Sunan Ibn Majah, Book 1, Hadith 304]
- **At conjugal relations:** *“O Allah, keep Satan away from us and keep Satan away from what You have provided us.”* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 67, Hadith 5165]
- **At sleeplessness:** *“O Allah, the stars have set and the eyes have grown still, and You are the Ever-Living, the Sustainer; neither slumber nor sleep overtakes You. O Ever-Living, O Sustainer, grant my night tranquility and give my eyes sleep.”* [Sunan al-Bayhaqī]
- **At the close of prayer:** *“Whoever glorifies Allah thirty-three times after every prayer, and praises Allah thirty-three times, and magnifies Allah thirty-three times, and completes the hundred by saying: ‘There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah alone, without partner; to Him belongs sovereignty and to Him is all praise, and He is over all things competent’ — his sins will be forgiven, even if they are like the foam of the sea.”* [Sahih Muslim, Book 5, Hadith 597]
- **At the close of a gathering:** *“Glory be to You, O Allah, and praise be to You. I bear witness that there is no god but You; I seek Your forgiveness and turn to You in repentance.”* [Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Book 5, Hadith 3433]
- **When boarding a vehicle:** *“All praise is for Allah who has subjected this to us, and we could not have otherwise subdued it; and indeed to our Lord we will return.”* [Qur’an, Az-Zukhruf 43:13–14]
- **When traveling:** *“O Allah, in You I seek support, and by You I move about, and by You I travel. O Allah, I ask You in this journey of mine righteousness and piety, and action that pleases You. O Allah, make easy for us this journey of ours and fold up its distance for us. O Allah, You are the Companion on the journey and the Guardian over the family. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the hardship of travel, from the sadness of sight, and from an evil return in wealth, family, and children.”* [Sahih Muslim, Book 31, Hadith 2661]
- **When rain falls:** *“O Allah, make it a beneficial downpour,”* two or three times. [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 59, Hadith 1032]
- **When hearing thunder:** *“O Allah, do not kill us with Your wrath, nor destroy us with Your punishment, and pardon us before that.”* [Sahih Muslim, Book 48, Hadith 2735]
- **Upon sighting the new moon:** *“Allah is the Greatest! O Allah, bring it upon us with safety and faith, well-being and Islam, and guidance to what You love and are pleased with. My Lord and Your Lord is Allah.”* [Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Book 4, Hadith 3451; Sunan ad-Dārimī]
- **When congratulating on marriage:** *“May Allah bless you and bless upon you, and bring you together in goodness.”* [Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Book 11, Hadith 1091]
- **Upon seeing a child:** *“I seek refuge for you in the complete words of Allah from every devil and poisonous creature, and from every envious eye.”* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 78, Hadith 6384]
- **At worry and grief:** *“There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.”* [Qur’an, Al-Anbiyā’ 21:87]
- **When visiting the sick:** *“O Allah, remove the harm, Lord of the people. Heal, for You are the Healer; there is no healing except Your healing, a healing that leaves no trace of sickness.”* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 76, Hadith 5745; Sahih Muslim, Book 36, Hadith 2191]
- **When offering condolences for a death:** *“Indeed, to Allah belongs what He took, and to Him belongs what He gave, and everything with Him has a fixed term; so be patient and seek reward.”* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 81, Hadith 6420]
- **In the funeral prayer:** *“O Allah, forgive him, have mercy upon him, pardon him, grant him security, overlook his faults, honor his dwelling, expand his entrance, and wash him with water, snow, and hail. Purify him of his sins as a white garment is purified of dirt. And replace for him a home better than his home, a family better than his family, and a spouse better than his spouse. Admit him to Paradise and protect him from the punishment of the grave and from the punishment of the Fire.”* [Sahih Muslim, Book 11, Hadith 963]

That I be a Muslim.
