# Chapter 5: Overcoming My Ego

## That I Triumph over My Self

Man is in conflict with his *nafs* (نفس) — his self — until he triumphs over it, or it triumphs over him, or the conflict remains ongoing, the battle ebbing and flowing, until death overtakes him while he is still in that state: *"And [by] the soul and He who proportioned it, and inspired it [with discernment of] its wickedness and its righteousness. He has succeeded who purifies it, and he has failed who instills it [with corruption]."* [Qur'an, Surah Ash-Shams 91:7-10].

To this meaning the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, alluded when he said: *"Trials are presented to hearts like a reed mat, straw by straw. As for any heart that absorbs them, a black spot is dotted in it; and as for any heart that rejects them, a white spot is dotted in it, until hearts become one of two kinds: a white heart like a smooth stone, harmed by no trial; and another black and inverted, recognizing no good and rejecting no evil."* [Sahih Muslim, Book of Faith, Hadith 144].

### Three Categories of People in the Battle of the Self

People, in the battle of the *nafs*, are of three categories.

**First**, a category whose desires have triumphed over them, so they inclined to the earth and settled into worldly life. These are the disbelievers and those who followed their path of those whom Allah forsook and so their own selves caused them to forget Him. Allah the Exalted describes them in His Book: *"Have you seen he who has taken as his god his [own] desire, and Allah has sent him astray due to knowledge and has set a seal upon his hearing and his heart and put over his vision a veil? So who will guide him after Allah? Then will you not be reminded?"* [Qur'an, Surah Al-Jathiyah 45:23].

**Second**, a category who struggle against their selves and wrestle with their desires. They triumph at times and are defeated at others. They err and then repent; they disobey Allah and then regret and seek forgiveness: *"And those who, when they commit an immorality or wrong themselves [by transgression], remember Allah and seek forgiveness for their sins — and who can forgive sins except Allah? — and [who] do not persist in what they have done while they know."* [Qur'an, Surah Aal-E-Imran 3:135]. To these the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, alluded when he said: *"Every son of Adam is a sinner, and the best of sinners are those who repent."* [Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Book of Repentance, Hadith 2499].

It is narrated in this regard from Wahb ibn Munabbih that he said: Iblis met Yahya ibn Zakariyya, peace be upon them both. Yahya said to him: Tell me about the dispositions of the son of Adam in your view. Iblis said: They are of three kinds. A kind among them are like guarded kings; we have no power over them at all. And a kind who are in our hands like a ball in the hands of your children, and sometimes they suffice themselves against us. The third kind are the most difficult of the categories upon us: we pounce upon one of them until we attain what we need from him, then he rushes to *istighfar* (استغفار) — seeking forgiveness — and spoils by it what we had attained from him. So neither do we despair of him, nor do we attain our need from him.

### Supports of Victory in the Battle of the Self

#### The Heart

That the *qalb* (قلب) — the heart — be alive, tender, pure, firm, and radiant, according to the statement of Ali ibn Abi Talib, may Allah be pleased with him: *"Indeed, Allah has vessels upon His earth, and the vessels of Allah are the hearts. The most beloved of them to Him are the most tender, the purest, and the firmest."* Then he explained, saying: *"The firmest in religion, the purest in certainty, and the tenderest toward brothers."* And his statement: *"The heart of the believer is bare, with a shining lamp within it; and the heart of the disbeliever is black and inverted."* [Recorded by Ahmad and al-Tabarani].

The Noble Qur'an depicts the hearts of the believers, saying: *"The believers are those whose hearts tremble when Allah is mentioned, and when His verses are recited to them, they increase them in faith."* [Qur'an, Surah Al-Anfal 8:2]. And it depicts the hearts of the disbelievers, saying: *"So indeed, it is not the eyes that are blind, but blind are the hearts which are within the breasts."* [Qur'an, Surah Al-Hajj 22:46]. And He says: *"Then do they not reflect upon the Qur'an, or are there locks upon [their] hearts?"* [Qur'an, Surah Muhammad 47:24].

#### The Intellect

That the *aql* (عقل) — the intellect — be discerning, perceptive, discriminating, and guided by the sciences through which nearness to Allah is attained and His greatness and power are realized. It is the basis of the statement of the Exalted: *"Only those fear Allah, from among His servants, who have knowledge."* [Qur'an, Surah Fatir 35:28].

The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, pointed to the value of this blessing when he said: *"Allah has not created any creature more noble to Him than the intellect."* And his statement to Ali ibn Abi Talib, may Allah be pleased with him: *"When people draw near to Allah through various acts of righteousness, draw near to Him through your intellect."* And his statement: *"A man has not acquired anything like the excellence of an intellect that guides its possessor to guidance and turns him away from misguidance."* [Recorded by Ibn Hibban and others].

For this reason Islam drove people toward knowledge and understanding, and toward deep comprehension of religion, so that the intellect may take from causes what helps it to distinguish between good and evil, right and wrong, lawful and unlawful. The Messenger of Allah said: *"Whoever Allah intends good for, He grants him understanding of the religion."* [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Knowledge, Hadith 71]. And he said: *"The virtue of the scholar over the worshipper is like my virtue over the least of my companions."* [Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Book of Knowledge, Hadith 2685]. All of this is because of the value and effect of knowledge in deepening faith in souls and acquainting man with the realities of this universe.

Thus the believer's intellect is an aware worker that distinguishes between good and evil, lawful and unlawful, right and wrong, because in it he looks by the light of Allah from behind a thin veil: *"And he for whom Allah has not appointed light, for him there is no light."* [Qur'an, Surah An-Nur 24:40].

Nothing extinguishes the light of the intellect except sins, persistence in them, open commission of them, and failure to repent from them, according to the statement of the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him: *"Whoever commits a sin and parts with his intellect, it never returns to him."* And his statement: *"Were it not that the devils hover over the hearts of the sons of Adam, they would behold the dominion of the heavens and the earth."* [Recorded by Ahmad and others].

It is narrated from Anas ibn Malik, may Allah be pleased with him, that he said: *"When I entered upon Uthman, may Allah be pleased with him, I had met a woman on my way and looked at her askance and contemplated her beauty. Uthman said when I entered: One of you enters while the trace of fornication is upon his eyes. Do you not know that the fornication of the eyes is looking? Repent, or I shall punish you. I said: Was this revealed after the Prophet? He replied: No, but it is insight, evidence, and true perspicacity."* [Recorded by Abu Nu'aym in *Hilyat al-Awliya*].

### Manifestations of Psychological Defeat

When man's heart dies or hardens, and when his intellect is extinguished or deviates — indeed, when he is defeated in his battle with *Shaytan* (الشيطان) — the entryways of evil into his self multiply, especially since *Shaytan* courses through the son of Adam like the coursing of blood.

When his resistance and psychological immunity are shattered, *Shaytan* becomes his constant companion: *"Satan has overcome them and made them forget the remembrance of Allah."* [Qur'an, Surah Al-Mujadilah 58:19]. To this meaning the noble verse alludes: *"[Iblis] said, 'Because You have put me in error, I will surely sit in wait for them on Your straight path. Then I will come to them from before them and from behind them and on their right and on their left.'"* [Qur'an, Surah Al-A'raf 7:16-17].

Moreover, the most dangerous disease that afflicts the defeated is *waswasah* (وسوسة) — insinuation: *Shaytan* whispers to them in every matter of their lives to turn them away from the path of Allah. In this regard the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said: *"Indeed, Satan lies in wait for the son of Adam at the paths. So he lay in wait for him at the path of Islam and said: Will you submit and leave your religion and the religion of your forefathers? So he disobeyed him and submitted. Then he lay in wait for him at the path of *hijrah* (الهجرة) and said: Will you emigrate? Will you leave your land and your sky? So he disobeyed him and emigrated. Then he lay in wait for him at the path of *jihad* (الجهاد) and said: Will you strive? It is but the waste of life and wealth: you will fight and be killed, your wives will be married by others, and your wealth will be divided. So he disobeyed him and strove. Then the Messenger of Allah said: Whoever does that and dies, it is incumbent upon Allah to admit him to Paradise."* [Sunan an-Nasa'i, Book of Jihad, Hadith 3097]. How good it would be if the reader-brother would review the story of *Shaytan* and the monk of the Children of Israel in the exegesis of the statement of the Exalted: *"Like Satan when he said to man, 'Disbelieve.' But when he disbelieved, he said, 'Indeed, I am disassociated from you. Indeed, I fear Allah, Lord of the worlds.'"* [Qur'an, Surah Al-Hashr 59:16].

### Means of Protection from Satan's Entryways

Indeed, in order to help man confront the satanic challenges and the diabolical suggestions of *Iblis* (إبليس), Islam directed him to many things that help him stand firm in the battle and enable him to overcome the most hostile of his enemies.

One of the righteous summarized them when he said: *"I looked and reflected: from which door does Satan come to man? I found that he comes through ten doors:

- **First:** greed and bad assumption — so I confronted him with trust and contentment.
- **Second:** love of life and long hopes — so I confronted him with fear of the sudden surprises of death.
- **Third:** love of blessing and fear of bad reckoning — so I confronted him with gratitude and contentment.
- **Fourth:** self-admiration (*al-ujb*) — so I confronted him with humility and fear of the consequence.
- **Fifth:** belittling people and showing them little respect — so I confronted him with recognizing their rights and sanctity.
- **Sixth:** envy — so I confronted him with contentment and satisfaction with Allah's distribution among His creation.
- **Seventh:** showing off and people's praise — so I confronted him with sincerity.
- **Eighth:** stinginess — so I confronted him with the perishing of what is in creatures' hands and the permanence of what is with the Creator.
- **Ninth:** arrogance — so I confronted him with contentment."*

Among the directives that Islam emphasized as a way to ward off the arrows of *Iblis* and his schemes is *dhikr* (ذكر) — the remembrance of Allah — at the beginning of every action. Abu Hurayrah narrated in this regard the following: *"The devil of the believer met the devil of the disbeliever. The devil of the disbeliever was fat, fleshy, and powerful, while the devil of the believer was emaciated, disheveled, dusty, and humiliated. The devil of the disbeliever said to the devil of the believer: Why are you so emaciated? He said: I am with a man who, when he eats, mentions the name of Allah, so I remain hungry; and when he drinks, mentions the name of Allah, so I remain thirsty; and when he puts on clothing, mentions the name of Allah, so I remain naked; and when he anoints himself, mentions the name of Allah, so I remain disheveled. The devil of the disbeliever said: And I am with a man who does none of that, so I share with him in his food, drink, and clothing."* [Musnad Ahmad].

Among the means of protection is avoiding satiety and overeating, even if the food is lawful and pure, according to the statement of the Exalted: *"Eat and drink, but be not excessive."* [Qur'an, Surah Al-A'raf 7:31]. And His statement, exalted be He: *"Indeed, Satan runs through the son of Adam like the running of blood, so narrow his passageways with hunger."* [Musnad Ahmad].

Among them also are recitation of the Qur'an, remembrance of Allah, and *istighfar* — seeking forgiveness — according to his statement: *"Indeed, Satan places the tip of his snout on the heart of the son of Adam. When he remembers Allah, he withdraws; and when he forgets Allah, he devours his heart."* [Ibn Abi al-Dunya, *al-Mujabbin*].

Among them is dispelling haste and deliberating over matters, according to his statement: *"Haste is from Satan and deliberateness is from Allah."* [Sunan Abu Dawud, Book of Behavior, Hadith 4606].

The space is too narrow to mention all the causes, works, and counsels that Islam enjoined for protection against the ambushes and schemes of *Shaytan*. And Allah the Exalted spoke truly when He said: *"Indeed, those who fear Allah — when an impulse touches them from Satan, they remember [Him]; and suddenly they have insight."* [Qur'an, Surah Al-A'raf 7:201].
