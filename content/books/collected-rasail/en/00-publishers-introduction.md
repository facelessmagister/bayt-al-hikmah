# Publisher's Introduction

A quarter of a century has now passed since the martyrdom of the striving Imam, Ḥasan al-Bannā — may God illuminate his face — yet his letters, speeches, and works remain a radiant light by which generations of believing intellectuals are guided, and from which they draw a pure Islamic culture suffused with a luminous, wholesome spirituality.

Since that time, the letters of the martyred Imam have appeared in numerous editions from various publishing houses, in different forms and at different levels, most of them below the standard required for book-printing and publication, whether to reduce costs for the sake of readers, or to compete and gain more material profit. All of these editions, praise be to God, have been exhausted. Many of them, moreover, were tied to circumstances whose time has passed, and there is no longer any benefit in reprinting them.

For this reason, the heirs of the martyred Imam — may God have mercy on him — saw that the time had come to reconsider this matter, to organize these works, and to add what the martyred Imam had left behind but which circumstances had not allowed him to publish before his death, as well as what should properly be added to some of what had already been published for the sake of thematic unity, or published separately when its subject stands on its own.

Accordingly, the legal heirs completed the gathering of these printed works and confined them within a single collection, entrusting its distribution and reprinting to a single body, in a new, unified production that preserves the legitimate rights of the heirs and safeguards the standing of these works.

The heirs of the martyred Imam did not intend by this any material gain; rather, their purpose was to organize these works and put an end to the tampering and trafficking in them. The heirs of the martyred Imam entrusted Dār al-Qurʾān al-Karīm with the task of distributing these publications, so that it would be the sole authorized distributor among those holding legitimate rights over what had previously been printed.

This edition, which we have been commissioned to reprint photographically from the last edition without alteration or change, is a temporary edition necessitated by the present need of readers after that edition was exhausted, until the collected works of the martyred Imam are reprinted in the new, desired edition.

Our Lord, grant us success.

*Dār al-Qurʾān al-Karīm*
