# Epistle 6: A Brief Note on the Excellence of Night Prayer, Supplication, and Seeking Forgiveness

Praise be to God, Lord of the Worlds, and prayers and peace be upon our master Muḥammad, the Imam of the God-fearing, and upon his family and his Companions, and whoever follows his guidance and calls to his mission until the Day of Judgment.

To proceed: Among the litanies of the striving brothers was that they would gather one night each week for familiarity, brotherhood, remembrance, and supplication. I wished to offer them this concise note on the excellence of night prayer (*qiyām al-layl*), supplication (*duʿāʾ*), seeking forgiveness (*istighfār*), and related matters, together with a selection of transmitted prayers, as a reminder of the Prophetic etiquettes and guidance in the manner of performing them. They had intended thereby solace and joy; but I intend thereby reminder and exhortation. What lies between a servant and his Lord is too vast to be contained in a book. I ask God for sincerity, a good ending, and success, and prayers and peace be upon our master Muḥammad and upon his family and his Companions.

## The Excellence of Night Prayer and the Hours of Intimate Converse

O my brother: when you seek the times of intimate converse, when your Lord is alone with you while people are asleep, and when the eyes have grown calm; when the universe has become still, the night has let down its curtains, and its stars have set — then bring your heart to attention, remember your Lord, reflect upon your weakness and the greatness of your Master, so that you come before Him in humility and your heart is reassured by His remembrance, rejoicing in His nearness and mercy, weeping from awe of Him, rolling up your sleeves, persisting in supplication, striving in seeking forgiveness, and spending the hours of the night in what no jinn nor man sees, for His command when He wills a thing is only to say to it, "Be," and it is. Ask Him for your sins, your faults, your shortcomings, your worries, your wealth, your safety, your family, your spouse, your children, your brothers, and your sisters, for victory is from none but God, the Mighty, the Wise.

And why, O my brother, when there are so many verses and transmitted reports concerning the excellence of these hours, the purification of these times, and the urging of the believers from the very beginning to seize them and hasten to acts of obedience? And why, O my brother, were the righteous predecessors so eager to attain the tremendous merit found in these hours, for at these times they were repentant, worshipping, praising, remembering, standing, bowing, prostrating, sitting, receiving bounty from their Lord and seeking nearness to Him, asking forgiveness and beseeching the One whose favor is immense and who is a Generous One to be asked? Among what they recited are the words of the Almighty:

> They are not all the same; among the People of the Book is an upright community that recites the verses of Allah throughout the night and prostrates. They believe in Allah and the Last Day, and enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong, and hasten to good deeds. Those are among the righteous. And whatever good they do — never will it be removed from them. And Allah is Knowing of the righteous. [Qur'an, Aal-E-Imran 3:113-115]

> Gardens of perpetual residence beneath which rivers flow, purified spouses, and approval from Allah. And Allah is Seeing of His servants — those who say, "Our Lord, indeed we have believed, so forgive us our sins and protect us from the punishment of the Fire," the patient, the true, the obedient, those who spend, and those who seek forgiveness before dawn. [Qur'an, Aal-E-Imran 3:15-17]

> Establish prayer from the decline of the sun until the darkness of the night and the Qur'an of dawn. Indeed, the recitation of dawn is ever witnessed. And from the night arise for prayer with it as additional worship for you; it is expected that your Lord will resurrect you to a praised station. [Qur'an, Al-Isra 17:78-79]

> And the servants of the Most Merciful are those who walk upon the earth in humility, and when the ignorant address them, they say words of peace, and those who spend the night bowing and prostrating before their Lord. [Qur'an, Al-Furqan 25:63-64]

> Is one who is devoutly obedient during periods of the night, prostrating and standing, fearing the Hereafter and hoping for the mercy of his Lord, like the disbeliever? Say, "Are those who know equal to those who do not know?" Only those with understanding will take heed. [Qur'an, Az-Zumar 39:9]

> So be patient over what they say, and exalt with praise of your Lord before the rising of the sun and before its setting, and from the night exalt Him and after prostration. [Qur'an, Qaf 50:39-40]

> Indeed, the righteous will be among gardens and springs, accepting what their Lord has given them. Indeed, they were before that doers of good. They used to sleep but little of the night, and in the hours before dawn they would ask forgiveness. [Qur'an, Adh-Dhariyat 51:15-18]

> And remember the name of your Lord and devote yourself to Him with complete devotion — the Lord of the East and the West; there is no deity except Him, so take Him as Disposer of your affairs. And be patient over what they say, and depart from them with a graceful departure. And leave Me with the matter of the deniers, those of ease, and give them respite for a little. [Qur'an, Al-Muzzammil 73:8-11]

> Indeed, your Lord knows that you stand almost two thirds of the night or half of it or a third of it, as do a party of those with you. And Allah determines the night and the day. He has known that you will not be able to count it and has turned to you in forgiveness, so recite what is easy of the Qur'an. [Qur'an, Al-Muzzammil 73:20]

> Indeed, We sent the Qur'an down during the Night of Decree. And what can make you know what the Night of Decree is? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months. The angels and the Spirit descend therein by permission of their Lord for every matter. Peace it is until the emergence of dawn. [Qur'an, Al-Qadr 97:1-5]

> So remember the name of your Lord and devote yourself to Him with complete devotion. The Lord of the East and the West; there is no deity except Him, so take Him as Disposer of your affairs. And be patient for what they say, and avoid them with gracious avoidance. [Qur'an, Al-Muzzammil 73:8-10]

> So be patient over what they say, and exalt with praise of your Lord before the rising of the sun and before its setting, and during the night glorify Him and after prostration. [Qur'an, Qaf 50:39-40]

> So remember the name of your Lord and devote yourself to Him with complete devotion. [Qur'an, Al-Muzzammil 73:8]
