# Honest Counsel

O Muslim Brothers — especially you who are enthusiastic and hasty:

Receive from me a high, ringing word from this pulpit in the mosque of this conference. Your path is one whose bounds are laid down and established. I am not one who imposed these bounds; I am fully convinced that they are the safest path to the goal. Yes, it may be long, but there is no other. True manhood is shown in patience, experience, effort, and constant work. Whoever among you wishes to hasten a revolution before it ripens, or to pluck a flower before its season, I am not with him in that at all. Let him turn from this call to another of the calls. But whoever is patient with me until the seed grows, the tree branches, the fruit ripens, and the harvest season comes — his reward in that is with Allah. We shall not miss, together with him, the reward of the two goodly things: either victory and glory, or martyrdom and felicity.

O Muslim Brothers:

Adorn the movements of emotion with the looks of reason. Let the rays of reason penetrate the heart of emotion. Let your minds be bound by truthfulness of reality and fact. Uncover realities in the bright, shining light of reason. Do not seize upon every hope that flutters before you like a mirage. Do not collide with the laws of the universe, for they are overpowering; but overcome them, use them, harmonise their tempos, and seek help through some of them against others. Await the hour of victory, for it is not far from you.

O Muslim Brothers:

If you seek the face of Allah, and obtain His reward and pleasure, that is promised to you so long as you are sincere. Allah has not charged you with the results of actions, but He has charged you with sincerity of orientation and goodness of preparation. After that, if we err, we have the reward of striving workers; and if we are right, we have, in addition, the double reward of the successful. Experiences in the past and present have proven that there is no good except in your path, no deliverance except for the Ummah that treads your path, and no correctness except in what you do. So do not gamble with your lives, nor bet your standard of success and action. Allah is with you, and He will not let the workers' reward be lost. Allah does not waste the reward of those who do good. [Qur'an, At-Tawbah 9:120]
