# Epistle 2: The Muslim Brotherhood Under the Banner of the Qur’an

To the youth thirsting for the glory of Islam…

To the Ummah wandering at the crossroads…

To the caravan of the Qur’an, which has inscribed verses of pride upon the tops of epochs…

To every Muslim who hopes for sovereignty in this world and happiness in the abode of eternity…

We say:

Address of the Imām, the Martyr Ḥasan al-Bannā:
A message from the strong present to the bewildered present, and a gift from the honourable past to the radiant future.

O youth… O Imām seeking the life [of faith]…

O you who yearn for the victory of Allah’s religion… O you who advance your soul into the hands of your Lord…

Here is guidance and right direction; here is wisdom and correctness; here is the rapture of giving and the sweetness of *jihād*.

So hasten to the silent battalion, and work under the banner of the Master of the Prophets, and let the camp of the Muslim Brothers receive you:

> “And do not be like those who became divided and differed after the clear proofs had come to them.” [Qur’an, Āl ʿImrān 3:105]

## Muslim Brothers

In the name of Allah; all praise is due to Allah; and may Allah send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, and upon his family and Companions.

I love you with the love of Islam — a blessed, pure love from Allah — so peace, mercy, and blessings of Allah be upon you.

O Muslim Brothers!

O all people!

In this deafening clamour from the echo of the great, painful events which Allah has made the age traverse in this time, and in this torrential flood of calls that fill the corners of the universe and course through the waves of the ether in the depths of the unknown — furnished with all that beguiles and deceives of hopes, promises, and appearances — we advance our call, we the Muslim Brothers:

Calm — yet stronger than raging tempests.

Humble — yet more exalted than towering mountains.

Limited — yet broader than all these earthly lands together.

Free of false appearances and counterfeit adornment, yet surrounded by the majesty of truth, the sublimity of revelation, and the care of Allah.

Free of ambitions, desires, personal cravings, and individual gains, yet promising the believers and the sincere workers “sovereignty in this world and Paradise in the Hereafter.”

### In the Light of the First Call

O Muslim Brothers!

O all people!

Listen to it, clear and loud; let the voice of the First Caller resound in it after [so long], just as it resounded before.

> “Rise and warn, and your Lord glorify.” [Qur’an, Al-Muddaththir 74:2]

And with it echoes the secret of His saying, exalted is He:

> “So be patient over what they say and avoid them with gracious avoidance.” [Qur’an, Al-Muzzammil 73:10]

And the tongue of revelation speaks through it, addressing all people:

> “O mankind, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you, that you may become righteous — [He] who made for you the earth a bed [spread out] and the sky a ceiling, and sent down from the sky rain and brought forth thereby fruits as provision for you. So do not attribute to Allah equals while you know [that there is nothing similar to Him].” [Qur’an, Al-Baqarah 2:21–22]

What have we done for Islam?

O Muslim Brothers!

O all people!

Allah has sent for you an Imām, placed for you a system, perfected for you laws, revealed for you a Book, made lawful what is lawful, forbidden what is forbidden, and guided you to what contains your good and happiness, and this is the straight, even path. Have you then followed His Imām, honoured His system, executed His judgments, sanctified His Book, made lawful what He made lawful, and forbidden what He made forbidden?

Be honest in your answer, and you will see the truth clearly before you: all the systems upon which you walk in your worldly life are purely traditional systems, having no connection to Islam, deriving nothing from it, and depending upon nothing in it:

The system of internal government.

The system of international relations.

The system of judiciary.

The system of defence and military service.

The system of wealth and economy for the state and individuals.

The system of culture and education.

Indeed, the system of the family and the household.

Indeed, the system of the individual in his private conduct.

The prevailing spirit that dominates rulers and governments and shapes the phenomena of life in all their variety — all of this is far from Islam and the teachings of Islam.

And what remains after this?

These dilapidated mosques which the poor and the feeble visit, in which prayers die empty of spiritual meaning and humility, except through the guidance of Allah.

These days which are fasted during the year and become seasons of idleness, futility, eating, and drinking, in which no soul is renewed or spirit purified:

> “Except for those who believe and do righteous deeds; for them is a reward uninterrupted.” [Qur’an, Al-Bayyinah 98:7]

These deceptive appearances of baths, garments, beards, ceremonies, rites, words, and phrases — is this the Islam which Allah intended to be His great mercy and the supreme grace upon worlds?

Is this the guidance of the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, by which he wished to bring people out of darkness into light?

Is this the broad Qur’anic law that treated the ailments of nations, solved the problems of peoples, and laid down the most precise rules and firmest foundations for reform?

## The Wave of Western Imitation

O Muslim Brothers!

Indeed, O all people!

We must acknowledge that a strong, sweeping wave and a fierce torrent has overwhelmed minds and thoughts in a heedless span of time, while the Islamic nations were deluded by luxury and blessings, drowned in comfort and ease. Ideals and calls arose, systems and philosophies appeared, civilisations and cities were established, and all of this invaded the idea of Islam in the souls of its sons, attacked it in the heart of its homeland, surrounded it in every place, entered its lands uninvited, deceived them and seduced them; indeed, it occupied their hearts, minds, emotions, and inclinations. It had, in the means of misguidance, temptation, power, and dominance, what no other [civilisation] had before. It uprooted the whole of Islam, and nations that had been among the strongest of Islamic nations were deceived by it. What remained in them was heavily influenced, and a generation grew up in every Islamic nation closer to non-Islam [than to Islam]. They took charge of directing their affairs and occupied the seats of intellectual, spiritual, political, and executive leadership. They pushed the peoples, heedless, toward what they desired — nay, toward what they were used to — while the peoples did not know what was intended for them or what they would become. The voices of the callers to the dominant idea rose: “Purify what remains of Islam from Islam, cast Islam aside, accept with us, content and not reluctant, these standards, burdens, ideas, appearances, and manifestations. Cast away the residue of the sick idea from your minds and souls, and do not be deceitful hypocrites, obstinate: you do the work of the Westerners while you speak the words of the Muslims.”

We must know that we have departed from the guidance of Islam and its foundations and principles. And Islam does not refuse that we take what is beneficial and adopt wisdom wherever it is found. But it utterly refuses that we imitate the disbelievers in everything, that we replace Allah’s religion with something, that we cast aside His creeds, obligations, limits, and judgments, in order to run after a people whom the world has seduced and devils have allured.

Indeed, science has advanced, art has advanced, thought has advanced, hopes have multiplied, the world has adorned itself, the earth has taken its ornaments and become beautiful, and people have lived in luxury and delight. But has any of this brought them happiness? Has any of it given them the sweetness of this life, or led to peace and tranquillity in their souls?

Have hearts rested on their beds?

Have eyes dried of tears?

Has crime been fought, and have societies rested from the evils of criminals?

Have the poor become free of need, and have the hungry bellies in the outskirts of cities been filled?

Have these diversions and amusements that fill space and move like the wailing of mourners brought consolation to the grieving?

Have peoples tasted the flavour of rest and calm, and have they become secure from the terrors of wars?

None of this, O people. So what then is the superiority of this civilisation over…?

And is this all?

Do we not see these systems, teachings, and philosophies even among nations and in the most civilised peoples, destroying one another, annihilating one another, so that people, after long experience and great sacrifice, return with the bitterness of failure, the death of hope, and the pain of deprivation?

What, then, is our task, we the Muslim Brothers?

As a whole, it is to stand in the face of this overwhelming wave of material civilisation that has removed us from the leadership of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, and the guidance of the Qur’an, deprived the Ummah of the lights of his guidance, and delayed its progress by hundreds of years, until it recedes from our lands and leaves our people a people without worth. We will not stop at this limit; rather, we will pursue it in its own lands and invade it in the heart of its home. The whole world has the right to call out in the name of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, and the whole earth to rejoice at the teachings of the Qur’an, and the shade of Islam to spread far and wide upon the earth, so that the religion is entirely for Allah:

> “He is the one who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth to manifest it over all religion, although those who associate others with Allah dislike it. It is He who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth to prevail it over all religion. Sufficient is Allah as Witness. O you who have believed, shall I guide you to a transaction that will save you from a painful punishment? [It is that] you believe in Allah and His Messenger and strive in the cause of Allah with your wealth and your lives. That is best for you, if you should know. He will forgive for you your sins and admit you to gardens beneath which rivers flow and pleasant dwellings in gardens of perpetual residence. That is the great attainment. And [you will obtain] another [favour] that you love — victory from Allah and an imminent conquest; and give good tidings to the believers.” [Qur’an, Al-Fatḥ 48:28–29; As-Ṣaf 61:9; At-Tawbah 9:111]

This is our task, we the Muslim Brothers, in general. As for some of its details, it is, first, that in Egypt — which is at the forefront of the lands and peoples of Islam, and likewise in others — there should be:

An internal system of government in which the word of Allah, exalted is He, is realised:

> “And judge, [O Muḥammad], between them by what Allah has revealed and do not follow their inclinations and beware of them, lest they tempt you away from some of what Allah has revealed to you… And We have revealed to you, [O Muḥammad], the Book in truth so that you may judge between the people by that which Allah has shown you. And be not for the deceitful an advocate.” [Qur’an, Al-Māʾidah 5:49, 5:48]

And a system of practical judiciary derived from the noble verse:

> “But no, by your Lord, they will not [truly] believe until they make you, [O Muḥammad], judge concerning that over which they dispute among themselves and then find within themselves no discomfort from what you have judged and submit in [full, willing] submission.” [Qur’an, An-Nisāʾ 4:65]

And an independent economic system for wealth, money, the state, and individuals, based on His saying, exalted is He:

> “And do not give the weak-minded your property, which Allah has made a means of sustenance for you…” [Qur’an, An-Nisāʾ 4:5]

And a system of culture and education that eliminates ignorance and darkness, and matches the majesty of revelation in the first verses of the Book of Allah:

> “Read in the name of your Lord who created — created man from a clinging substance.” [Qur’an, Al-ʿAlaq 96:1–2]

And a system for the family and the household that raises the Muslim boy, the Muslim girl, and the Muslim man, in which the word of Allah, exalted is He, is realised:

> “O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones…” [Qur’an, At-Taḥrīm 66:6]

And a system for the individual in his private conduct, following the success meant by the saying of Allah, exalted is He:

> “He has succeeded who purifies it.” [Qur’an, Ash-Shams 91:9]

And a general spirit that dominates every individual in the Ummah, whether ruler or ruled, based on the saying of Allah, exalted is He:

> “But seek, through that which Allah has given you, the home of the Hereafter; and [yet], do not forget your share of the world. And do good as Allah has done good to you. And desire not corruption in the land. Indeed, Allah does not like corrupters.” [Qur’an, Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:77]

We want: the Muslim individual… and the Muslim household…

And the Muslim people… and the Muslim government…

And the state that leads the Islamic states, unites the scattered Muslims, restores its glory to them, returns to them their lost lands, their violated homelands, and their usurped countries, then carries the *jihād* and the banner of the call to Allah, until the world is made happy by the teachings of Islam.

## Our Equipment

That is our goal, O people, and this is our *manhaj*! What is the equipment for the realisation of this *manhaj*?

Our equipment is the equipment of our predecessors before us. The weapon with which our leader and our model, the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, and his Companions conquered the world, despite their small number and scant resources and the magnitude of their effort, is the weapon we shall bear to conquer the world anew. They believed in the mightiest, strongest, most sacred, and most everlasting faith: faith in Allah and His victory and support:

> “If Allah should aid you, no one can overcome you…” [Qur’an, Āl ʿImrān 3:160]

And in the *manhaj* and its excellence and soundness:

> “There has come to you from Allah a light and a clear Book.” [Qur’an, Al-Māʾidah 5:15]

And in brotherhood and its rights and sanctity:

> “Indeed, the believers are brothers.” [Qur’an, Al-Ḥujurāt 49:10]

And in recompense and its majesty, greatness, and magnificence:

> “That is because they are not afflicted by thirst or fatigue or hunger in the cause of Allah, nor do they tread on any ground that enrages the disbelievers, nor do they inflict upon an enemy any infliction but that it is registered for them as a righteous deed. Indeed, Allah does not allow to be lost the reward of the doers of good.” [Qur’an, At-Tawbah 9:120]

And in the destiny of the community chosen by Allah to save the world and to whom He has written favour thereby; they were the best nation brought forth for mankind.

They responded to the Caller calling to faith and believed. We hope that Allah will grant us the same faith and beautify it in our hearts as He beautified it in theirs, and as He beautified it in their hearts before.

Faith, first, is our equipment.

And they knew, more truly than any nation and more firmly, that this call is not realised except by sacrifice, giving, advancing the soul, and wealth. They advanced their souls, gave their lives, and strove in Allah’s cause until they met Him, while the voice of the Merciful called to them:

> “Allah has certainly fulfilled His Messenger’s vision in truth. You will surely enter al-Masjid al-Ḥarām, if Allah wills, in safety, with your heads shaved and [hair] shortened, not fearing. He knew what you did not know and has arranged before that a conquest near [at hand]. It is He who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth to manifest it over all religion, although those who associate others with Allah dislike it.” [Qur’an, Al-Fatḥ 48:27–28]

One of them hurried forth, responding to the caller, leaving everything dear behind, going to Allah without provision:

> “And [there is a share for] those who came after them, saying, ‘Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith and put not in our hearts [any] resentment toward those who have believed. Our Lord, indeed You are Kind and Merciful.’” [Qur’an, Al-Ḥashr 59:10]

And another advanced with the sword upon his neck:

> “Indeed, Allah has purchased from the believers their lives and their properties [in exchange] for that they will have Paradise. They fight in the cause of Allah, so they kill and are killed. [It is] a true promise [binding] upon Him in the Torah and the Gospel and the Qur’an. And who is truer to his covenant than Allah? So rejoice in your transaction which you have contracted. And it is that which is the great attainment.” [Qur’an, At-Tawbah 9:111]

That is how they were: true *jihād*, great sacrifice, immense giving. And thus we strive to be:

> “Go forth, whether light or heavy, and strive with your wealth and your lives in the cause of Allah. That is better for you, if you only knew.” [Qur’an, At-Tawbah 9:41]

And they established prayer and commanded the right and forbade the wrong:

> “And let there be [arising] from you a nation inviting to [all that is] good, enjoining what is right and forbidding what is wrong, and those will be the successful.” [Qur’an, Āl ʿImrān 3:104]

## Between Imagination and Reality

Those who hear this will say that it is pure imagination, empty talk, and delusion. How can these people, possessing nothing but faith and struggle, resist these combined, arrogant powers, these varied and different weapons, and restore their rights from between the arms of the hordes of spears?

Many will say this, and they have some excuse, for they have despaired of themselves and of the power of the Mighty, the Able. But we say that the reality in which we believe and for which we work is the truth. We read the word of Allah, exalted is He:

> “O you who have believed, if you support Allah, He will support you and plant firmly your feet. But those who disbelieve — for them is misery, and He will waste their deeds.” [Qur’an, Muḥammad 47:7–8]

Even if those [disbelievers] have opened the lands of the world, and even if the enemies of Allah in the lands were more numerous than our predecessors and possessed greater equipment, they were believers and *mujāhidūn*. We shall confront them as the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, confronted them when he said: “This matter shall continue until the rider travels from ʿAdan to Ḥaḍramawt fearing nothing but Allah and the wolf [for his sheep].” At that time they were weak, and on the day of the Pledge of ʿAqabah, Suraqah ibn Mālik was promised the bracelets of Kisrā, while he was a pursuer of his religion with neither riding-camel nor companion. And on the day [the Prophet] stood overlooking the white palaces of the Byzantines, besieged by the polytheists in his city from above and below:

> “And [remember] when the disbelievers plotted against you to restrain you or kill you or evict you [from Makkah]. But they plan, and Allah plans. And Allah is the best of planners.” [Qur’an, Al-Anfāl 8:30]

Then what happened after that?

The centuries inclined their ears to the call of the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him; verses of the Qur’an reverberated through time; suns of guidance shone in every place from the hearts of his Companions and followers; light filled the world; peace spread over the earth; humanity tasted the sweetness of happiness through the brotherhood of faith and the security of peoples under the shade of this first generation of the pupils of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him. The palaces of the Byzantines were opened, the cities of Persia submitted, the earth stretched out its necks, cast off its burdens, and its corners were smoothed; it yielded submissively to the saving guidance, breathing in the breaths of prophethood and mingling them with the breaths of holy revelation, surrounded by the mercy of Allah from every side:

> “It is He who sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers that they would increase in faith along with their [present] faith. And to Allah belong the soldiers of the heavens and the earth, and ever is Allah Knowing and Wise. [He will] admit them to gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide. That is the great attainment. And those who disbelieved — their forces will not avail them at all. So do not slacken and call for peace while you are superior; and [remember], Allah is with you and will never deprive you of [the reward of] your deeds. The life of this world is only amusement and diversion, but if you believe and fear Allah, He will give you your rewards, and will not ask you for your properties. If He should ask you for them and press you, you would withhold, and He would expose your unwillingness. Here you are — those invited to spend in the cause of Allah — but among you are those who withhold. And whoever withholds only withholds from himself; and Allah is the Free of need, while you are the needy. And if you turn away, He will replace you with another people; then they will not be the likes of you.” [Qur’an, Al-Fatḥ 48:4–5, 48:11]

We shall confront the people today with this [same] period, and we shall conquer as our predecessors conquered in the near yesterday. And victory is not except from Allah, the Mighty, the Wise. And Allah’s promise will surely be fulfilled:

> “And We desired to show favour unto those who were oppressed in the earth, and to make them leaders and to make them the inheritors, and to establish them in the land…” [Qur’an, Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:5–6]

> “So be patient. Indeed, the promise of Allah is truth. And let them not impel you to impatience those who do not believe.” [Qur’an, Ar-Rūm 30:60]

## If We Had a Government

If we had an Islamic government sound in Islam, sincere in faith, independent in thought and organisation, aware of the greatness of the trust in its hands and the majesty of the Islamic system it has inherited, believing that in it lies the healing of peoples and the guidance of mankind together, then we could ask it to support the world with the principles of Islam, and to demand of other states that they study and examine it, and drive them toward it through repeated calls, persuasion, evidence, successive missions, and other means of calling and communication. It would thereby acquire a spiritual, political, and scientific standing among other governments, and would be able to renew the life of the people, push them toward guidance and light, and rouse in their souls earnestness, seriousness, and work.

It is strange that you find a people with a state that it glorifies, calls to, spends for, and compels people toward; and you see Fascism and Nazism made sacred by their peoples, struggled for, boasted of in following, and all social systems subordinated to their teachings. And you see the social and political doctrines that are empty [of spirit] find eager supporters who spend their souls, minds, thoughts, pens, money, newspapers, and efforts upon them, living and dying for them.

And it is fitting that an Islamic government should perform the duty of calling to Islam — which gathers all the good qualities of these systems and casts away their evils — and present it to other peoples as a universal system containing the correct, clear solution and a binding obligation, and most incumbent upon the Muslims as peoples and groups before these systems perish and before the system of calls is known:

> “And let there be [arising] from you a nation inviting to [all that is] good, enjoining what is right and forbidding what is wrong, and those will be the successful.” [Qur’an, Āl ʿImrān 3:104]

But woe to our rulers for this! We have all grown up in the laps of foreigners, adopted their ideas, followed their traces in rivalry for their pleasure. We would not be exaggerating if we said that the idea of independence in managing affairs and works perhaps does not occur to them, let alone that it becomes a clear *manhaj*. We have previously presented this wish to many people in Egypt, and it would have been natural that this presentation have an effect. For a people that has lost Islam in itself, its homes, its private and public life, is incapable of presenting it to others or advancing with a true call to it: one who lacks a thing cannot give it.

This is not the task of the Muslim Brothers, for experience has proven their absolute inability to establish it. It is the task of this new generation. So call it well, be serious in forming it, accustom it to independence of soul and heart, independence of thought and opinion, independence of *jihād* and struggle; fill its ardent spirit with the beauty of Islam and the sublimity of the Qur’an; enlist it under the banner of Muhammad and his call. And soon you will see from it the living miracle that strives against itself and brings happiness to others.

## The Nature of Our Idea

O Muslim Brothers!

Indeed, O all people!

We are not a political party, even though politics on the foundations of Islam is part of our idea. We are not a charitable reformist association, even though doing good and reform are among our greatest objectives. We are not a sports team, even though bodily and spiritual training is one of our means. We are none of these formations, for all of them are created for a limited, local goal, for a fixed period, and their composition suggests nothing more than a desire to form a group and to bear administrative titles.

But we, in Islam, are an idea and a creed, a system and a *manhaj*; not confined by locality, restricted by race, separated by a barrier of tongues, nor ended by the death of a leader. That is because it is the system of the Lord of the Worlds and the *manhaj* of His trustworthy Messenger.

We are, O people — and that is no boast — the heirs of the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, and the bearers of his banner after him. We raised his banner as they raised it, spread his message as they spread it, preserved his Qur’an as they preserved it, and gave glad tidings of his call as they gave glad tidings. And mercy is for the doers of good, and we are among them, Allah willing.

This is your rank, so do not belittle yourselves, comparing yourselves to others, or follow in your call a path other than the path of the believers, or weigh your call — which takes its light from the light of Allah and its *manhaj* from the tongue of His Messenger — against other calls that are created by necessities and carried away by events and days. You have called and struggled, and you have seen, through this good effort, voices rising that call for the leadership of the Messenger of Allah and the dominance of the Qur’anic system, and the necessity of practical revival, and pure blood flowing from pure, noble youth in the paths of Allah, and a sincere desire for martyrdom in His cause. This is a success beyond what you expected. So continue your efforts, act, listen, and your highest matters will not be neglected.

Whoever follows us now has already gained the company [of the righteous]; whoever lags behind us today among the sincere will catch up with us tomorrow. For the foremost is the foremost, and whoever turns away from our call out of indifference, mockery, belittlement, or despair of victory — time will make clear to him the magnitude of his error, and Allah will cast truth upon falsehood in his heart until it becomes clear:

> “And this is My path, which is straight, so follow it; and do not follow [other] ways, for you will be separated from His way. This has He instructed you that you may become righteous.” [Qur’an, Al-Anʿām 6:153]

We have entrusted you to the Guardian, and the Guardian is sufficient.

## Marching the Battalions

He whose right is clear arrays his legions and prepares for the decisive stand.
So array the battalions like lions and strike the houses of falsehood.

The Prophet of eternity has come, and we have a pledge with Allah that we shall fulfil;
We have gone to Allah by the path of struggle and the way of noble sacrifice;
And a people who knock on the gates of hope,
Guiding to the truth by a sight that sees,
For it there is no fear of the blame of any blamer.

He whose right is clear arrays his legions and prepares for the decisive stand.
So let the castles tremble and the dwellings of falsehood be struck;
Until upon the dust we offer our souls — a brotherhood whose foundation is the fear of the Merciful.

Wounds are the ransom of the great hopes,
By which they are guided, by certitude, by guidance,
Delicate when the sorrow visits,
Running to the aid of the free,
And fierce lions when they don the garment of battle,
Calling to the standard of Islam: Here we are!

He whose right is clear arrays his legions and prepares for the decisive stand.
So array the battalions like lions and strike the houses of falsehood.

Brother of the Qur’an, seek the highest rank;
Here is the brother to be followed;
And what the concealment conceals,
We shall requite with fright when it is exposed;
Then We shall make it taste the worst of life.

So let not the spirit of Allah be sold,
And let the riders halt at the lowest point,
Until victory in the decisive stand,
Until victory in the decisive stand.
