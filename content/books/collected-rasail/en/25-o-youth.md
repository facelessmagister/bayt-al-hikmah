# Epistle 6: O Youth

> "Say, 'I warn you only of one thing — that you stand for Allah, [seeking Him] in pairs and individually, and then give thought.' There is no madness in your companion. He is only a plain warner to you before a severe punishment. Say, 'Whatever payment I might have asked of you — it is not yours; my payment is only from Allah, and He is, over all things, a Witness.' Say, 'Indeed, my Lord projects the truth, [He is] Knower of the unseen.' Say, 'The truth has come, and falsehood has departed; indeed falsehood is bound to depart.' Say, 'If I should err, I only err against myself. But if I am guided, it is by what my Lord reveals to me. Indeed, He is Hearing and Near.'" [Qur'an, Sabaʾ 34:46-50]

## The Call of the Muslim Brethren — or, The Call of Islam in the Fourteenth Century Hijrī

O youth:

A thought does not succeed unless it finds people who believe in it, sincerity in working for it, perseverance in pursuing it, and readiness to sacrifice and labor for its realization. These four pillars — faith, sincerity, perseverance, and sacrifice — are almost the peculiar characteristics of youth. For the basis of faith is the intelligent heart; the basis of sincerity is the pure conscience; the basis of perseverance is the strong feeling; and the basis of sacrifice is the youthful determination. All these qualities are found only in youth. Hence youth have been, in every age and every Ummah that has risen, the source of its awakening, the life of every rising, the bearers of every idea: "[They are] young men who believed in their Lord, and We increased them in guidance." [Qur'an, Al-Kahf 18:13]

Hence your duties are many, your responsibilities great, your Ummah's rights upon you multiplied, and the trust upon your necks heavy. Hence you must think deeply, learn much, define your position, advance to rescue, and give the Ummah a complete picture of this youth.

A young man may grow up in a tranquil, settled Ummah with stable authority and flourishing civilization, and so turn to himself more than to his Ummah, seeking diversion and amusement with a calm soul and untroubled conscience. Or he may grow up in a struggling, laboring Ummah that has been overwhelmed by foreigners and whose affairs have been shackled by enmity; then it strives as much as it is able in the paths of struggle to recover the stolen homeland, the plundered heritage, the lost freedom, the sublime ideals, and the guiding examples. In that case it is among the most obligatory duties upon that young man that he turn to his Ummah more than to himself. In doing so he wins the good of this world in the field of struggle, and the good of the Hereafter from the reward of Allah. And how good is our fortune that we belong to the second group, for our souls have been opened to an Ummah ever striving and continuously fighting in the path of truth and freedom. Take courage, then, for victory is near for the believers, and how great is the success of those who depend upon Allah.

O youth:

Perhaps one of the gravest aspects of a rising Ummah, while it is in the dawn of its awakening, is the multiplicity of calls, the confusion of voices, the variety of programs, the divergence of plans and paths, and the abundance of those who claim leadership and command. All of this is division of efforts and scattering of strength, by which the desired goals become difficult to reach. Hence the study of these calls and comparing them with one another is an essential matter for whoever desires reform.

Therefore, it is my duty to explain to you, briefly and clearly, the call of Islam in the fourteenth century of the Hijrah.

### The Call of the Muslim Brethren

O youth:

We believe — and in this there is no argument or doubt — and we hold a creed firmer than mountains and deeper than the secrets of conscience: that there is no single idea that will save the tormented world, guide perplexed humanity, and lead people to the straight path, and that is therefore worthy that one sacrifice in proclaiming it and preaching it to people with lives, wealth, and all that is cheap and dear. This idea is "upright Islam" (*al-Islām al-Ḥanīf*), for which Allah has testified that there is no deity worthy of worship except He, and the angels, and those possessed of knowledge, standing firm in justice: "There is no deity worthy of worship except He, the Exalted in Might, the Wise. Indeed, the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam." [Qur'an, Āl ʿImrān 3:18-19]

> "This day I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you and have approved for you Islam as religion." [Qur'an, Al-Māʾidah 5:3]

Our idea is thus purely Islamic. It rests upon Islam, draws from it, strives for it, and works in the paths of exalting its word. It does not bargain with Islam as a system, is not content with anything other than it as an ideology, and does not obey anyone else in anything:

> "And whoever desires other than Islam as religion — never will it be accepted from him." [Qur'an, Āl ʿImrān 3:85]

There has come upon Islam and the Muslims a period in which events have followed one upon another and disasters have succeeded one another; the enemies of Islam have united to extinguish its light, conceal its signs, mislead its sons, paralyze its movements, weaken its soldiers, and distort its teachings and rulings — at times by omitting from them, at others by adding to them, and at still others by interpreting them wrongly. They were aided in that by the loss of Islamic political authority, the tearing apart of its universal caliphate, and the demobilization of its armies, so that it became a despised, colonized Ummah in the grip of unbelief.

So the first of our duties, we the Muslim Brethren, is to show the people the picture of this Islam: clear, complete, and evident, without addition, omission, or ambiguity. That is the theoretical part of our idea. And we must demand its realization point by point, urge them to carry it out, and take from them the pledge of action for this idea.

In all of that we rely upon the Book of Allah, which falsehood cannot approach from before it or from behind it; and the sound, established Sunnah of His Messenger; and the pure life-trace of the righteous predecessors of this Ummah. We seek from none of that except the pleasure of Allah, the discharge of the trust, the guidance of mankind, and the direction of people.

We will struggle in the path of realizing our idea, and we will fight for it as long as we live, and we will call all people to it, and we will sacrifice everything in its path. We will live with it nobly or die for it nobly. Our slogan will always be: "Our Lord is Allah, and the Messenger is our leader, the Qurʾān is our constitution, jihad is our path, and death in the path of Allah is the highest of our aspirations."

Allah has honored you, in relation to Himself, by helping you and raising you upon His religion, and has written for you thereby the rank of precedence in the world and the station of leadership among the nations and the honor of the teacher among his students:

> "You are the best nation produced [as an example] for mankind. You enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong and believe in Allah." [Qur'an, Āl ʿImrān 3:110]

> "And thus We have made you a median [i.e., just] community that you will be witnesses over the people." [Qur'an, Al-Baqarah 2:143]

The first thing this calls you to is that you believe in yourselves, know your station, and believe that you are the masters of the world even if your enemies wish humiliation for you, and the teachers of the nations even if they have overcome you outwardly in worldly life, for the final outcome is yours.

So define, O youth, your unity. Define your purposes and your goals. The first of strength is unity, and the result of unity is the great, manifest victory. So believe, be brothers, work, and then expect victory:

> "And give good tidings to the believers." [Qur'an, Al-Baqarah 2:223]

The whole world is in turmoil, and every system in it has failed to cure it. There is no remedy for it except Islam. So advance, in the name of Allah, to its rescue. Do not wait for a savior; there will be no savior except the message of Islam, which you carry as a torch and with which you illuminate.

O youth:

The method of the Muslim Brethren has defined stages and clear steps. We know exactly what we want, and we know the means to the realization of this will.

1. First, we want the Muslim man in his thought and creed, in his character and emotion, in his action and conduct. This is our individual formation.

2. Then we want the Muslim family in its thought and creed, in its character and emotion, in its action and conduct; and by this we mean the woman as we mean the man, and we mean childhood as we mean youth. This is our family formation.

3. Then we want the Muslim people in all of that as well; and in this we work to make our call reach every home, our voice heard in every place, our idea seen and penetrating into villages, hamlets, towns, centers, cities, and capitals. We spare no effort in that and leave no means untried.

4. Then we want after that the Muslim government, which leads this people to the mosque and carries them upon the guidance of Islam as the Companions of the Messenger of Allah and Abū Bakr and ʿUmar carried them before. In this we do not recognize any governmental system unless it rests upon the foundation of Islam and derives from it; we do not recognize these political parties or these traditional forms that the people of unbelief and the enemies of Islam forced us to adopt and work within. We will work for the abolition of this system in all its manifestations and the formation of the Islamic government on the basis of this system.

5. Then we want after that that every part of our Islamic homeland that foreign greed has divided and European ambitions have scattered be returned to us. We do not recognize these political divisions, nor do we surrender to these international agreements that have turned the Islamic homeland into weak, torn mini-states to be swallowed by the usurpers. We will not be silent about the freedom of these peoples and the tyranny of others over them. Egypt, Syria, Iraq, the Ḥijāz, Yemen, Tripoli, Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco, and every inch of land in which a Muslim says "There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah" — all of that is our great homeland that we strive to liberate, save, deliver, and rejoin its parts to one another.

If the German Reich imposes itself as protector of all in whose veins flows German blood, then Islamic solidarity requires every strong Muslim to consider himself a protector of all whose soul has drunk the teachings of the Qurʾān. There is no scope in the custom of Islam for the Egyptian worker to be stronger in bond than the Iraqi worker. Solidarity is everything in Islam; and is not Iraq but love and hatred?

6. Then we want after that that the banner of Allah be raised high over those lands that were honored by Islam long ago, in whose air the voices of the callers to Allah resounded with takbīr and tahlīl, then the evil of fate willed that their light be eclipsed and they return to unbelief after Islam. So al-Andalus, Sicily, the Balkans, southern Italy, and the islands of the Mediterranean Sea are all Islamic colonies that must return to the embrace of Islam, and it is necessary that the White Sea and the Red Sea again become Islamic lakes as they once were.

If Mussolini has the right to revive the Roman Empire — and this supposed empire was built upon nothing but greed and caprice — then we have the right that the Islamic Caliphate, which stood upon justice, equity, spreading light, and guiding people, be revived.

7. Then, together with that, we want to spread our call throughout the world and reach all people, and make it touch the horizons of the earth, and subject every tyrant to it, so that there will be no more tribulation and religion will all be for Allah:

> "On that day the believers will rejoice in the victory of Allah. He gives victory to whom He wills, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Merciful." [Qur'an, Ar-Rūm 30:4-5]

Each of these stages has its steps, branches, and means, but this is not the place for lengthy elaboration. Allah is the one sought for help, and He is sufficient for us and the best Disposer of affairs.

The timid and cowardly will say that this is an impossible dream and a delusion that has overcome the souls of these people. That is the weakness that we do not recognize and that Islam does not know. It is the feebleness that was cast into the hearts of this Ummah and became a source of strength for its enemies. It is the ruin of the heart from within, and it is the disease that caused the fall of the Muslims. We declare, in clearness and frankness, that every Muslim who does not believe in this program and does not work for its realization has no share in Islam; let him seek another idea to follow and another program to serve.

O youth:

You are not weaker than those before you by whom Allah realized this program. So do not weaken, do not grieve, and place before your eyes the word of your Lord:

> "Those to whom people said, 'Indeed, the people have gathered against you, so fear them.' But it [merely] increased them in faith, and they said, 'Sufficient for us is Allah, and [He is] the best Disposer of affairs.'" [Qur'an, Āl ʿImrān 3:173]

We will educate ourselves so that the Muslim man may arise from us; we will educate our families so that the Muslim family may arise from them; we will educate our people so that the Muslim people may arise from them. From among this Muslim people, the Muslim government will arise. Then we will proceed step by step until we complete the course and reach the goal that Allah has set for us, not the one we have set for ourselves. We will reach it, Allah willing, by His permission; and Allah refuses except to complete His light, even though the disbelievers dislike it.

We have prepared for that a faith that is not shaken, a knowledge that is not arrested, a confidence in Allah that is not weakened, and souls that prefer to meet Allah as martyrs in His path.

Let all of that be removed from the field of internal and external politics; for we derive all of it from Islam, and we know that this separation between religion and politics is not from the teachings of upright Islam and is not recognized by the sincere Muslims in their religion, their spirit, and their conduct. So whoever wants to turn us away from this program is either an enemy of Islam or ignorant of it, and he has no way but one of these two positions.


# Epistle 6: O Youth

## Correct Conceptions of the Muslim Brotherhood

He is mistaken who thinks that the Muslim Brotherhood are a company of dervishes who have confined themselves to a narrow circle of Islamic devotions—nothing but prayer, fasting, remembrance, and glorification. The first Muslims did not know Islam in this image, nor did they call to it in this manner; rather, they believed in it as creed and worship, homeland and nationality, character and morality, culture and law, clemency and strength. They believed it to be a complete system that imposes itself upon every manifestation of life and regulates the affairs of this world as it regulates the Hereafter. They believed it to be a system of knowledge and spirit together; thus to them it is religion and state, Qurʾān and sword.

Yet with all this, they do not neglect the care of their worship, nor do they fall short in performing their obligatory duties. Rather, they strive to perfect their prayer, recite the Book of Allah, and remember Allah—the Exalted and Glorified—in the manner He commanded. These are the bounds they set for themselves, without negligence or excess, for they know best the words of the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him:

> "Indeed this religion is firmly rooted; so penetrate into it gently, for the innovator does not traverse the earth nor leave any trace upon its back." [Aḥmad]

And despite this, they take from their worldly life only the share that does not harm their Hereafter, knowing the words of Allah—the Exalted and Glorified:

> "Say, 'Who has forbidden the adornment of Allah which He has produced for His servants and the good things of provision?'" [Qur'an, Al-Aʿrāf 7:32]

The Muslim Brethren know that the best description of their group is the description of the Companions of the Messenger of Allah: "Monks by night and knights by day." And likewise they strive to be the foremost helpers.

He is also mistaken who thinks that the Muslim Brethren disdain the homeland and patriotism; for the Muslims are the most sincere of people to their homelands and the most devoted in serving them, honoring every one who works for them sincerely. You have learned to what extent they go in their patriotism and to what dignity they cling for their Ummah.

But the difference between the Muslims and other advocates of narrow nationalism is that the basis of the Muslims' patriotism is the Islamic creed. They work for a homeland such as Egypt and struggle in its cause, and issue religious opinions concerning this struggle, because Egypt is part of the land of Islam and the leader of the Ummah. Yet they do not stop at this feeling within its borders; rather, they feel with it every Islamic land and every Islamic homeland. Meanwhile every mere nationalist stops at the borders of his nation and feels no obligation to support the homeland except through imitation, appearance, ostentation, or self-interest—not through the obligation laid down by Allah upon His servants.

It suffices as a proof of the patriotism of the Muslim Brethren that they hold a firm, binding creed that negligence regarding any inch of land inhabited by a Muslim is an unforgivable crime until they restore it or perish without restoring it. There is no salvation for them from the punishment due to it.

He is likewise mistaken who thinks that the Muslim Brotherhood are callers to laziness or neglect; for the Brethren declare at every moment that Islam must be a leader in everything, and they are not content with anything less than leadership, sovereignty, struggle, and precedence in everything—in knowledge, in power, in health, and in wealth. Falling behind in any of these areas is harmful to our idea and contrary to the teachings of our religion. At the same time, we reject from people this overwhelming materialism that adorns for them the desire to provide only for themselves, so that they devote their talents, times, and efforts to personal selfishness; thus no one works for another anything, nor cares for any matter concerning his Ummah. The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

> "Allah has prescribed excellence in all things." [Muslim]

He is likewise mistaken who thinks that the Muslim Brotherhood are advocates of racial or class division within the Ummah. We know that Islam has shown the keenest care in strengthening the general human bond among the children of Adam, as He—the Exalted—says:

> "O mankind, indeed We have created you from male and female and made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another." [Qur'an, Al-Ḥujurāt 49:13]

It came for the good of all people and as a mercy from Allah to the worlds:

> "And We have not sent you, [O Muḥammad], except as a mercy to the worlds." [Qur'an, Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:107]

Its mission, more than any other religion, is far from dividing hearts and stirring up hatred. In this spirit the Qurʾān affirms and praises this unity, as in His words:

> "The Messenger has believed in what was revealed to him from his Lord, and [so have] the believers. All of them have believed in Allah and His angels and His books and His messengers, [saying], 'We make no distinction between any of His messengers.'" [Qur'an, Al-Baqarah 2:285]

Islam has forbidden aggression even in moments of anger and confrontation, as He—the Exalted—says:

> "O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm for Allah, witnesses in justice, and do not let the hatred of a people prevent you from being just. Be just; that is nearer to righteousness." [Qur'an, Al-Māʾidah 5:8]

And He enjoined kindness and excellence among fellow citizens even if their creeds and religions differ:

> "Allah does not forbid you from those who do not fight you because of religion and do not expel you from your homes— from being righteous toward them and acting justly toward them." [Qur'an, Al-Mumtaḥanah 60:8]

And He enjoined fairness toward those who are at peace with you and good dealings with them: "They have what we have, and upon them is what is upon us." [Aḥmad]

With all this, we do not call to racial division or sectarian partisanship. But alongside this, we do not purchase this unity at the expense of our faith, nor do we bargain over our creed for its sake, nor do we harm the interests of the Muslims for its sake. We buy it only with mercy, fairness, and justice, and that suffices. Whoever seeks otherwise, we shall stop him at his limit and close to him the door he wished to enter, for:

> "And to Allah belongs [all] honor, and to His Messenger, and to the believers." [Qur'an, Al-Munāfiqūn 63:8]

He is likewise mistaken who thinks that the Muslim Brethren work for the sake of some hidden affair among affairs, or rely upon one group among groups. The Muslim Brethren work for their own goal, guided by their Lord, by Islam, and by its adherents in every time and place. They spend of what Allah has provided them, seeking His pleasure, and they boast that until now they have not drawn near to anyone, nor sought help from any individual, faction, or group.

### To the Youth

Upon these firm principles and toward these sublime teachings we call you, O youth. If you believe in our idea, follow our footsteps, and travel with us the path of upright Islam (*al-Islām al-Ḥanīf*)—if you rid yourselves of every thought besides that and devote for your creed every excellence of good—then the good of this world and the Hereafter is yours. Allah will accomplish through you, if He wills, what He accomplished through your predecessors in the First Era; and every sincere worker among you in the field of Islam will find what satisfies his soul and fully occupies his activity, if he is among the sincere.

But if you refuse except to waver, to be shaken, and to hesitate between wandering calls and failed methodologies, then the army of Allah will advance, caring neither for you nor for numbers:

> "And victory is not except from Allah, the Exalted in Might, the Wise." [Qur'an, Āl ʿImrān 3:126]

— Ḥasan al-Bannā
