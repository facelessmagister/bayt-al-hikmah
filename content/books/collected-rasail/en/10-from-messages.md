# From the Messages of the Muslim Brothers

## The Goal and the Means

I think, O virtuous brothers, that you have learned from this long speech the goal of the Muslim Brothers, their means, and their mission. The goal of the Muslim Brothers is limited to: forming a new generation of believers, with correct Islamic teachings, who work; giving the Ummah the complete Islamic colouring in every aspect of its life — "the colouring of Allah; and who is better than Allah in colouring?" [Qur'an, Al-Baqarah 2:138]. Their means in that are limited to changing the general custom and cultivating the supporters of the call upon these teachings until they become a model for others in adhering to them and being eager for them, and in submitting to their rulings. When they walked toward their goal within the bounds of their means, they reached a degree of success at which they are content and for which they praise Allah. I think I am not in need of further explanation in this regard.
