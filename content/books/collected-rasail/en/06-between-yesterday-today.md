# Epistle 3b: Between Yesterday and Today — On the Meaning of Islam, the Characteristics of the Call, and Positions on Force, Government, the Constitution, the Law, Unity, and the Caliphate

The people have mixed with the Muslim Brothers and have become acquainted with them; they did not stop at the limits of mere hearing, nor did they cast upon the Muslim Brothers any accusation they could imagine. Rather, they came to know their reality and understood everything about their call, in belief and in action.

For this reason I wished to speak before you, briefly, about the meaning of Islam and its vivid image in the souls of the Muslim Brothers, so that the foundation to which we call, to which we are proud to belong, and from which we seek support, may be clear and manifest.

## (1) The Comprehensive Meaning of Islam

We believe that the rulings and teachings of Islam are comprehensive, regulating the affairs of people in this world and in the Hereafter. Those who think that these teachings concern only the devotional or spiritual side, to the exclusion of other sides, are mistaken in this opinion. For Islam is creed and worship, homeland and citizenship, religion and state, spirituality and action, Qur'an and sword. The Noble Qur'an proclaims all of this and regards it as the very core of Islam. It commands *iḥsān* (excellence) in all of it together. To this the noble verse alludes:

> "But seek, through that which Allah has given you, the home of the Hereafter; and [yet], do not forget your share of the world. And do good [iḥsān] as Allah has done good to you." [Qur'an, Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:77]

You read in the Qur'an and in the prayer, if you wish, the words of Allah the Exalted regarding creed and worship:

> "And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, [being] sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth, and to establish prayer and to give zakah. That is the correct religion." [Qur'an, Al-Bayyinah 98:5]

And you read His words regarding judgement, politics, and governance:

> "But no, by your Lord, they will not [truly] believe until they make you, [O Muḥammad], judge concerning that over which they dispute among themselves and then find within themselves no discomfort from what you have judged and submit in [full, willing] submission." [Qur'an, An-Nisā' 4:65]

And you read His words regarding debt and commerce:

> "O you who have believed, when you contract a debt for a specified term, write it down. And let a scribe write [it] between you in justice. Let no scribe refuse to write as Allah has taught him. So let him write and let the one who has the obligation dictate. And let him fear Allah, his Lord, and not leave anything out of it. But if the one who has the obligation is of limited understanding or weak or unable to dictate himself, then let his guardian dictate in justice. And bring to witness two witnesses from among your men. And if there are not two men [available], then a man and two women from those whom you accept as witnesses — so that if one of the women errs, then the other can remind her. And let not the witnesses refuse when they are called upon. And do not be [too] weary to write it, whether it is small or large, for its [specified] term. That is more just in the sight of Allah and stronger as evidence and more likely to prevent doubt between you, except when it is an immediate transaction which you conduct among yourselves. For [then] there is no blame upon you if you do not write it. And take witnesses when you conclude a contract. And let no scribe be harmed or any witness. For if you do so, indeed, it is [grave] disobedience in you." [Qur'an, Al-Baqarah 2:282]

And you read His words regarding *jihād*, fighting, and raiding:

> "And when you are among them and lead them in prayer, let a group of them stand [in prayer] with you and let them carry their arms. And when they have prostrated, let them be [in position] behind you and have the other group come forward which has not [yet] prayed and let them pray with you, taking precaution and carrying their arms. Those who disbelieve wish that you would neglect your weapons and your baggage so they could come down upon you in one [single] attack. But there is no blame upon you if you are inconvenienced by rain or are ill and put down your arms, but take precaution." [Qur'an, An-Nisā' 4:102]

And so on, among the brilliant verses dealing with these purposes, together with the rest of the general norms and social affairs.

Thus the Muslim Brothers connected themselves with the Book of Allah, relied upon it, and sought guidance in it. They became certain that Islam is this total, comprehensive meaning; that it ought to colour every atom of life, that life must be dyed with it, and that it must conform to its rulings, teachings, and derive from them — so long as the Ummah wishes to be Muslim in a correct Islamic way. But if it falls short in its worship and imitates non-Muslims in the rest of its affairs, then it is an Ummah deficient in Islam, like those of whom Allah the Exalted said:

> "Then do you believe in a part of the Scripture and disbelieve in a part? Then what is the recompense for those who do that among you except disgrace in worldly life; and on the Day of Resurrection they will be sent back to the severest of punishment. And Allah is not unaware of what you do." [Qur'an, Al-Baqarah 2:85]

## (2) The Sources of the Islamic Order

Beside this, the Muslim Brothers believe that the source of all Islamic teachings and their foundation is the Book of Allah, Blessed and Exalted, and the Sunnah of His Messenger, peace and blessings be upon him. If the Ummah holds fast to these two, it will never go astray. Many opinions and systems have attached themselves to Islam and inherited it, bearing the hue of the ages that produced them and the peoples that lived with them. Therefore it is necessary that the Islamic systems that will carry this Ummah draw from this pure, primordial spring — that is, that we understand Islam as the Companions and the Followers among the Righteous Predecessors understood it, may Allah be pleased with them all. We must stand firmly at these divine, prophetic roots, not measuring ourselves by what Allah has not burdened us with, nor conforming our age to the colour of an age that cannot keep pace with it. Islam is the religion of humanity.

## (3) Islam as a Universal Religion

Beside this also, the Muslim Brothers believe that Islam, as a universal religion, regulates every aspect of life for people in all nations and communities, in all ages and epochs. It came complete and exalted, not presented to the partialities of this or that circumstance, especially not to purely worldly matters. Rather, it comprises the universal principles in every one of these affairs and guides people to the practical method of applying them and walking within their bounds, so as to guarantee correctness and soundness in this application — or at least effort toward it.

Islam has paid complete attention to the treatment of the human soul, which is the source of systems, the substance of thought, conception, and formation. It has prescribed for it effective remedies that purify it from disease, wash it from the filth of self-interest and ulterior motive, cleanse it of envy and rancour, and restrain it from oppression, deficiency, and aggression. When the soul is upright and sound, everything that proceeds from it becomes upright and sound. They say: justice is not in the text of the law but in the soul of the judge. If a complete and just law is brought before a judge driven by desires and ulterior motives, he will apply it in an oppressive manner in which there is no justice. But if a deficient and imperfect law is brought before a virtuous, just judge free from caprice and ulterior motives, he will apply it in a virtuous, just way full of goodness, righteousness, mercy, and fairness. Hence the human soul received great attention in the Book of Allah, and the first norms He formulated in this religion were a model of human conduct. All of this is part of the nature of Islam: it traverses ages and nations, rises to every purpose and demand. Thus Islam is not averse to benefiting from every sound system, provided it does not conflict with its universal principles and general foundations.

I do not wish, gentlemen, to be too lengthy in this explanation, for it is a broad field. These brief pointers are enough to cast light upon this general direction of the Islamic idea in the souls of the Muslim Brothers.

## The Idea of the Muslim Brothers Embraces Every Reformatory Meaning

It was a result of this general, comprehensive understanding of Islam among the Muslim Brothers that their idea embraced every side of reform in the Ummah, and included every element found in other reformatory thought. Every reformer, therefore, will find in the Muslim Brothers:

### (1) A *Salafī* Call

Because they call for returning Islam to its pure spring: the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger.

### (2) A *Sunnī* Method

Because they oblige themselves to act according to the pure Sunnah in everything, especially in creeds and transactions, leaving no path to that except through it.

### (3) A *Ṣūfī* Reality

Because they know that the foundation of spiritual experience lies in the rectitude of the soul, purity of heart, perseverance in action, renunciation of created things, love of Allah, and reliance upon the Hereafter.

### (4) A Political Body

Because they demand internal reform and the orientation of the Ummah's view toward other nations to the utmost degree of its capability.

### (5) A Sporting Club

Because they care for their bodies, knowing that the strong believer is better than the weak believer, and that the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Your body has a right over you." The duties of Islam cannot be performed completely and soundly except by a strong body — prayer, fasting, *ḥajj*, and *zakāh* all require a body able to bear the effort, work, and struggle to earn a livelihood. Accordingly, they have paid attention to the formation of their members and have organized sports with care that rivals, and often surpasses, clubs devoted solely to physical sport.

### (6) A General Cultural Association

Because Islam has made the seeking of knowledge an obligation upon every Muslim man and woman, and because the clubs of the Muslim Brothers are in reality schools of knowledge and culture, institutes for the cultivation of body, mind, and spirit.

### (7) An Economic Company

Because Islam is concerned with the management and earning of wealth, and because the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Good wealth is for the righteous man," and said: "Whoever spends the evening exhausted from the work of his hands has spent it forgiven by Allah," and "Allah loves the believer who is skilled [in a craft]."

### (8) A Social Idea

Because they are concerned with the diseases of the Islamic society and strive to find ways to treat it and heal the Ummah from them.

Thus we see that the comprehensiveness of the meaning of Islam has made our idea comprehensive for every side of reform, and has directed the activity of the Muslim Brothers to all these sides. While others turn to one side to the exclusion of others, they turn to all of them together, knowing that Islam demands them all together.

From this it happens that many aspects of the Muslim Brothers' work appear to people contradictory when they are not. They see the Muslim Brother in the *miḥrāb* humble, weeping, and supplicating; a short while later he is the same person, a preacher and teacher, striking ears with the alarms of exhortation; a short while later the same person is seen as a robust athlete playing football, training to run, or practising swimming; a short while later he is the same person in his shop or workshop, plying his craft with honesty and sincerity. These aspects may appear to people discordant, some not matching others. But if they knew that Islam gathers them all together, commands them, and urges them, they would realise that they are aspects of harmony and meanings of concord. It is because of this comprehensiveness that the Muslim Brothers have attracted every criticism and censure directed at these various sides. They have avoided bigotry for labels, since the comprehensive Islam gathers them around one name: the Muslim Brothers.
