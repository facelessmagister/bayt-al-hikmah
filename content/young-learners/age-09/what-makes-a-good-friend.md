## WHILE-READING (Read the Text)

### Part 1: What Is a Friend?

A friend is a person you like and enjoy spending time with. Friends play together. Friends talk together. Friends help each other. Everyone needs friends!

Meet Aki and Bo. Aki is nine years old. Bo is nine years old too. They go to the same school. They are in the same class. They are best friends.

One day, a new girl comes to their class. Her name is Lina. She does not know anyone. She feels shy. She sits alone at lunch.

### Part 2: Being a Good Friend

Aki sees Lina sitting alone. Aki says to Bo, "Lina looks sad. She is alone. Let us talk to her."

Bo says, "Good idea! We should include her. We can ask her to sit with us."

To include someone means to let them join in. Aki and Bo walk to Lina. Aki says, "Hi! Do you want to sit with us?"

Lina smiles. She says, "Yes, please! Thank you." She is happy. Aki and Bo are kind. Being kind means being nice and caring to other people.

At lunch, Lina has an apple. Aki has cookies. Bo has a sandwich. Aki says, "Do you want a cookie, Lina?" Lina says, "Yes! I will share my apple with you."

To share means to let someone use something that is yours. Sharing is a good thing to do. It shows you care.

### Check Your Understanding 2
- What does Aki say to Bo about Lina? _______________________
- What does "include" mean? _______________________
- What do the friends share at lunch? _______________________

---

### Part 3: Being Honest and Listening

After school, Aki and Bo play football with Lina. Lina kicks the ball. It goes over the fence. It breaks a flower pot.

Bo says, "Oh no! What should we do?"

Lina says, "We should tell the neighbour. We must be honest. Being honest means telling the truth. We cannot hide it."

Aki says, "You are right. It was an accident. We will say sorry."

They go to the neighbour's house. Lina says, "I am sorry. I kicked the ball and broke your pot. It was an accident." The neighbour says, "Thank you for being honest. That is brave. Do not worry. I can buy a new pot."

On the way home, Bo tells Lina about his dog. Lina listens. She does not talk over him. She does not look away. She listens. To listen means to hear what someone says and pay attention.

Bo says, "Thank you for listening, Lina. You are a good friend."

Lina smiles. She is not shy anymore. She has two new friends. Good friends share. Good friends are honest. Good friends listen. Good friends include everyone. Aki, Bo, and Lina will be friends for a long time.

### Check Your Understanding 3
- What does Lina break? _______________________
- What does Lina decide to do? _______________________
- Why does Bo say "You are a good friend" to Lina? _______________________
