## WHILE-READING (Read the Text)

### Part 1: What Is Anger?

Anger is a strong feeling. Everyone feels anger sometimes. It is normal. You are not bad for feeling angry. But what you do when you are angry is very important.

Think of anger like a volcano. A volcano is calm for a long time. It looks quiet. But deep inside, it gets hot. Pressure builds up. Then one day, it explodes. Lava and rocks fly everywhere. The volcano cannot control it.

Your anger can be like that. If you keep everything inside, it builds up. Then one small thing makes you explode. You might yell. You might say mean things. You might break something. That is why you must learn to handle anger before it explodes.

A trigger is something that makes anger start. A trigger can be a word, a look, or something that happens. For example, someone calls you a name. That is a trigger. Your face feels hot. Your hands make fists. Your heart beats fast. These are signs that anger is coming.

### Part 2: What to Do When Anger Comes

When you feel anger coming, the first thing to do is pause. To pause means to stop for a short time. Do not talk. Do not move. Just stop. Count to three in your head. One. Two. Three.

Then breathe. To breathe means to take air into your body. Breathe in slowly through your nose. Breathe out slowly through your mouth. Do this three times. When you breathe slowly, your body starts to calm down. Calm means quiet and relaxed.

Here is a trick. Smell the flower. Blow out the candle. Breathe in like you are smelling a flower. Breathe out like you are blowing out a candle. This helps your body feel calm.

After you breathe, you can think. Now you can ask yourself: Why am I angry? What happened? What can I do? When you are calm, you can think clearly. You can make a good choice.

### Part 3: Making Things Right

Sometimes anger makes us do things we should not. We might yell at a friend. We might say something mean. When that happens, we must apologize. To apologize means to say sorry for something you did wrong.

Meet Daniel. One day, his little sister breaks his model plane. It took him a week to build it. Daniel is very angry. His face feels hot. He yells at his sister. She cries.

Daniel's mother comes. She says, "Daniel, I know you are angry. It is okay to be angry. But it is not okay to yell. Let us breathe together."

Daniel and his mother breathe in slowly. They breathe out slowly. Daniel does it three times. His body starts to calm down.

Then Daniel thinks. He says, "I was angry because she broke my plane. But she is little. She did not do it on purpose."

Daniel goes to his sister. He says, "I am sorry I yelled at you. I was angry. But I should not have yelled. Can you forgive me?" She hugs him. She says, "I am sorry I broke your plane."

Daniel learned something important. Anger is normal. But we must pause, breathe, and think before we act. And when we make a mistake, we apologize. That is how we handle anger the right way.
