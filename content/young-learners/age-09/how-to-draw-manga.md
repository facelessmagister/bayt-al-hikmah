## WHILE-READING (Read the Text)

### Part 1: What Is Manga?

Manga is a style of comic art from Japan. The word "manga" means "fun pictures." Manga characters have big, bright eyes. They have fun hair and clear expressions. An expression is the look on a face. It shows a feeling, like happy, sad, or surprised.

You can draw manga too! You do not need to be perfect. You just need to practice. Every good artist started as a beginner. The most important thing is to have fun and keep drawing.

### Part 2: Getting Started

Before you draw, you need a pencil and paper. Start with a light sketch. A sketch is a quick, light drawing. You sketch first so you can fix mistakes easily. Do not press hard! Light lines are easy to erase.

First, draw a big circle. This will be the head. Then draw a line down the middle of the circle. This line helps you put the eyes in the right place. The eyes go on the middle line. In manga, eyes are big and round. They are the most fun part!

Next, draw the hair. Manga hair is spiky and fun. You can make it any shape you like. There is no wrong way to draw hair. Try pointy lines, curved lines, or wavy lines.

### Part 3: Making Your Character Come Alive

Now draw the outline. An outline is the outside line of your drawing. It makes the shape clear. Go over your best lines a little darker. You can use your pencil or a pen.

After the outline, you can shade. Shading means making some parts darker. Shade under the hair and under the chin. This makes your drawing look real. It gives your character shape. Do not shade everything! Only shade where shadows would be.

The best part is the expression. An expression shows how your character feels. Draw the eyebrows up for surprised. Draw them down for angry. Draw the mouth in a big smile for happy. You can change the expression and tell a whole story!

When you are done, your drawing is one panel. A panel is a box in a comic. You can draw more panels and make your own manga story. Each panel shows one moment. Put them together and you have a comic!
