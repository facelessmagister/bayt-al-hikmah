## WHILE-READING (Read the Text)

### Part 1: A Dog Is Missing

Maya is nine years old. She has a dog named Coco. Coco is a small brown puppy. She is only six months old. She likes to run and play.

One morning, Maya wakes up. She goes to the kitchen. She gives Coco her breakfast. Then Maya goes to brush her teeth. When she comes back, Coco is gone!

Maya looks in the living room. She looks in the garden. She looks under the bed. Coco is not there. The front door is open a little bit. Maya thinks Coco ran outside.

Maya feels very sad. She loves her puppy. She wants Coco to come home.

### Check Your Understanding 1
- What is the name of Maya's puppy? _______________________
- How old is Coco? _______________________
- Why does Maya think Coco is gone? _______________________

---

### Part 2: The Search

Maya tells her mother. Her mother says, "Do not worry. We will find Coco."

Maya and her mother walk around the neighbourhood. The neighbourhood is the area near their house. They see many streets and many houses. They ask people, "Have you seen a small brown puppy?"

An old man says, "Yes! I saw a puppy near the park."

Maya and her mother run to the park. Maya calls Coco's name. "Coco! Coco! Where are you?"

Then Maya hears a bark. A bark is the sound a dog makes. It is Coco! Maya runs toward the sound. She sees Coco under a big tree.

Coco is scared. She is shaking. Maya picks her up and holds her tight.

### Check Your Understanding 2
- Where does the old man see the puppy? _______________________
- What does Maya hear? _______________________
- Where is Coco? _______________________

---

### Part 3: A Happy Ending

Maya looks at Coco's collar. The collar is a band around Coco's neck. It has Coco's name on it. It also has Maya's phone number. Maya is happy. The collar helped people know who Coco belongs to.

Maya and her mother take Coco home. They give her water and food. Coco eats very fast. She was hungry!

Maya's mother says, "We must close the door carefully. Puppies like to explore."

Maya gives Coco a big hug. She is very happy that her puppy is home. She tells Coco, "Do not run away again!"

That night, Coco sleeps in Maya's bed. She is safe and warm. Maya smiles. She knows she will always take good care of her puppy.

### Check Your Understanding 3
- What is on Coco's collar? _______________________
- Why is Coco hungry? _______________________
- Where does Coco sleep at night? _______________________
