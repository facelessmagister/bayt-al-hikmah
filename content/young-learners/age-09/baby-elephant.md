## WHILE-READING (Read the Text)

### Part 1: A New Baby

In Africa, a mother elephant is going to have a baby. She waits for many months. Then, one morning, the baby comes out. It is a girl elephant. Her name is Lulu.

Lulu is a calf. A calf means a baby elephant. When she is born, she weighs one hundred kilograms. That is very heavy! A baby human only weighs about three kilograms. Lulu is as heavy as thirty baby humans!

Lulu's legs are very long, but they are also very weak. She tries to stand up. She falls down. She tries again. After one hour, she can stand. Her mother makes a happy sound.

### Check Your Understanding 1
- True or False? Lulu is a boy elephant. __________
- How much does Lulu weigh when she is born? _______________________
- How long does it take Lulu to stand up? _______________________

---

### Part 2: The Herd Helps

Lulu is not alone. She lives with a herd. A herd is a big family of elephants. There are mothers, aunts, sisters, and cousins. They all help Lulu.

The other elephants make a circle around Lulu and her mother. They want to protect the new calf. They do not want lions or hyenas to come near. The biggest elephants walk on the outside of the circle. The babies stay in the middle.

Lulu's aunt touches her with her trunk. The trunk is the elephant's long nose. Elephants use their trunks to pick up food, drink water, and say hello. Lulu's aunt uses her trunk to clean Lulu's face.

### Check Your Understanding 2
- What is a herd? (a) a lion / (b) a group of elephants / (c) a tree
- Why do the elephants make a circle? _______________________
- What does Lulu's aunt use to clean her face? _______________________

---

### Part 3: Lulu's First Day

Lulu is hungry. She drinks milk from her mother. She drinks a lot! A baby elephant drinks eleven litres of milk every day. That is more milk than you drink in a whole week.

After she drinks, Lulu wants to play. But the day is very hot. The sun is big and bright. Lulu's mother takes her to a place with mud. Lulu puts mud on her body. The mud keeps her cool. It also protects her skin from the sun.

At night, the herd stops walking. They find trees to sleep under. Lulu lies down next to her mother. She is very tired. It has been a big day for a small calf.

### Check Your Understanding 3
- How much milk does Lulu drink every day? _______________________
- Why does Lulu put mud on her body? _______________________
- Where does the herd sleep? _______________________

### Grammar Points
- Simple present tense for facts: "A calf means...", "Elephants use..."
- Simple past tense for narrative: "She fell down", "She tried again"
- Comparative: "very heavy", "very weak", "very tired"
- Numbers and measurements: "one hundred kilograms", "eleven litres"
- Time markers: "After she drinks", "At night", "one hour", "every day"
