## WHILE-READING (Read the Text)

### Part 1: Meet Bella

This is my cat. Her name is Bella.

Bella is big. Bella is soft.

Bella likes to sleep.

### Check Your Understanding 1
- Point to the cat. Is Bella big? Yes / No
- Does Bella like to sleep? Yes / No

---

### Part 2: Bella Plays

Bella likes to play.

Bella plays with a ball.

Bella runs. Bella runs fast!

### Check Your Understanding 2
- What does Bella play with? a ball / a book
- Does Bella run fast? Yes / No

---

### Part 3: Bella Sleeps

Now Bella is tired.

Bella sleeps on my bed.

Good night, Bella!

### Check Your Understanding 3
- Where does Bella sleep? on the bed / on the floor
- Is Bella tired? Yes / No

### Activity 2: Circle the Right Word

1. Bella is a (cat / dog).
2. Bella likes to (sleep / fly).
3. Bella plays with a (ball / shoe).
4. Bella is (big / small).

### Activity 3: Draw Your Pet
Draw a picture of your pet or a pet you like. Write one sentence about it.
