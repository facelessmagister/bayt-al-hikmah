## WHILE READING (Read the Text)

### Part 1: The Biggest Football Competition

Every four years, the world has a very big football competition. It is called the FIFA World Cup. Teams from many countries come to play. They all want to win. They all want to be the best team in the world.

In 2022, the World Cup was in Qatar. Qatar is a small country in the Middle East. It built eight new stadiums for the World Cup. The stadiums were beautiful. One stadium even looked like a tent in the desert.

### Check Your Understanding 1
- True or False? The World Cup is every two years. __________
- True or False? The 2022 World Cup was in Qatar. __________

---

### Part 2: The Final Match

Thirty-two teams played in the World Cup. They played many matches. In the end, two teams went to the final. The final match was on December 18, 2022.

The two teams were Argentina and France. Argentina had a famous player. His name was Lionel Messi. Many people say he is one of the best players ever. France also had a famous player. His name was Kylian Mbappé. He was very young and very fast.

The match was very exciting. Argentina scored first. Messi scored a goal. Then Argentina scored again. Argentina had two goals, and France had zero.

But then Mbappé scored two goals very fast! Now the score was two to two. The match was tied. Nobody knew who would win.

### Check Your Understanding 2
- Who scored for Argentina? _______________________
- Who scored for France? _______________________
- What was the score after Mbappé scored two goals? _______________________

---

### Part 3: The Champion

The final match went to extra time. Extra time means the players play more. Argentina scored again. Messi scored his second goal. Now Argentina had three goals.

But Mbappé scored again! He scored his third goal. Now the score was three to three. Mbappé had three goals. This is called a hat-trick. It is very special.

The match went to a penalty shootout. In a penalty shootout, each team takes turns to kick the ball at the goal. The goal keeper tries to stop it.

Argentina scored four penalties. France scored two penalties. Argentina won! The final score was four to two in the penalty shootout.

The Argentina players were very happy. They celebrated on the field. Messi held the big golden cup. He smiled and cried. After many years, he was finally a World Cup champion.

The people in Argentina celebrated too. They danced in the streets. They were very proud of their team. It was a night nobody in Argentina would forget.

### Check Your Understanding 3
- What does "hat-trick" mean? (a) three goals by one player / (b) three red cards / (c) three wins
- How did Argentina win? (a) more goals in the match / (b) penalty shootout / (c) more players
- Was Messi happy at the end? __________

### Grammar Points
- Simple past tense throughout (scored, went, was, had)
- Time markers (every four years, in 2022, on December 18, in the end, after many years)
- Numbers and scores (two to two, three to three, four to two)
- Comparative / superlative (the best, very exciting, very fast)
