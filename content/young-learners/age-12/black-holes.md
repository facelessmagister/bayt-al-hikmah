## WHILE-READING (Read the Text)

### Part 1: What Is a Black Hole?

A black hole is one of the strangest things in space. It is a place where gravity is so strong that nothing can escape from it — not even light.

But what is gravity? Gravity is the force that pulls things together. The Earth has gravity, and that is why you do not float away into space. The more mass something has, the stronger its gravity is.

A black hole has so much mass packed into a very small space that its gravity becomes incredibly strong. It pulls everything toward it like a giant cosmic vacuum cleaner. But it is not really a hole. It is a sphere of squeezed matter.

---

### Part 2: How Are Black Holes Made?

Scientists believe that black holes are made when very massive stars die. A star is a ball of burning gas. It shines because of the energy inside it. But stars do not last forever.

When a massive star gets very old, it runs out of fuel. Then it cannot keep burning. The star begins to collapse. It falls in on itself. All the matter in the star squeezes into a tiny, tiny space. This makes a black hole.

Not all stars become black holes. Only the most massive ones do. Our sun is not massive enough to become a black hole. When the sun dies, it will become a white dwarf — a small, dim star.

### Part 3: The Event Horizon and Beyond

If you get too close to a black hole, you cross a special line called the event horizon. Once you cross this line, you cannot go back. The gravity is too strong. Even light cannot escape from inside the event horizon. That is why black holes look black — no light can come out of them.

Scientists have never seen inside a black hole. It is invisible to our telescopes. But they can tell where black holes are by watching stars move around them. The stars orbit the black hole because of its strong gravity.

In 2019, scientists took the first-ever picture of a black hole. They did not see the black hole itself, of course. They saw the bright ring of hot gas around it. The picture was fuzzy, but it proved that black holes are real.

Albert Einstein predicted black holes more than one hundred years ago. His theory of relativity said that very massive objects could bend space and time. At first, many scientists did not believe black holes could exist. Now we know they do.

- Who predicted black holes over one hundred years ago? _______________________

### Part 4: Black Holes in Our Galaxy

Scientists think there is a very big black hole at the centre of our galaxy, the Milky Way. It is called Sagittarius A*. This black hole is millions of times more massive than our sun. But it is far away, so we do not feel its gravity.

There are also smaller black holes scattered across the galaxy. Scientists believe there are millions of them. Some are only a few kilometres wide, but they still have the mass of our sun.

Black holes are scary to think about, but they are also fascinating. They help scientists understand how the universe works. The study of black holes has led to new ideas about space, time, and the laws of physics.
